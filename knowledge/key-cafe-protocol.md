## Overview

Key Cafe is the centralized key and dealer plate management system at the Edmonton Office. Every key set and every dealer plate must flow through Key Cafe for every transaction — no exceptions, no shortcuts, no peer-to-peer transfers.

**Core principle:** "You signed it out" means you are responsible, period. Liability belongs to the signer until the item is checked back in through Key Cafe. There is no other defense.

---

## 1. Key Cafe — The Single Source of Truth

**Physical Location:** 3rd floor by Jorja's desk. [COMPLETE]

Key Cafe is the **only** system of record for key and dealer plate custody. At any moment, Key Cafe must accurately reflect:
- Which keys and plates are currently signed out
- Who signed them out
- When they were signed out

If a key or plate's location cannot be determined from Key Cafe, that is a compliance failure and must be investigated immediately.

---

## 2. Sign-Out Rules

### Who Can Sign Out
Any employee who has signed the Accountability Agreement may sign out keys or dealer plates through Key Cafe.

**Employees who have NOT signed the Accountability Agreement:**
- Cannot take a plate
- Cannot drive a dealership vehicle
- Cannot access Key Cafe for sign-out
- Cannot continue employment at the dealership without signing

### Sign-Out Process
1. Employee goes to Key Cafe
2. Employee signs out the key or plate under their name
3. Transaction is logged: employee name + timestamp
4. Employee takes the item

### No Exceptions
- No sign-outs without a logged entry
- No one takes a key or plate and "settles up later"
- No informal borrowing

---

## 3. Sign-In Rules

### When to Sign In
Keys and dealer plates must be signed back in through Key Cafe immediately when the employee is done with them — not at the end of the day, not when convenient, immediately.

### Sign-In Process
1. Employee returns to Key Cafe with the key or plate
2. Employee signs the item back in
3. Transaction is logged: employee name + timestamp

---

## 4. No Peer-to-Peer Transfers — Absolute Rule

**No employee may pass a key or dealer plate directly to another employee.**

If another employee asks you for a plate or key you currently have signed out, the answer is always:

> **"No — go back to Key Cafe, I'll check mine in, you sign it out."**

This is the exact language to use. There are no alternative acceptable responses.

### Why This Rule Exists
If an employee passes a plate to another employee without going through Key Cafe:
- The Key Cafe record still shows the first employee as responsible
- If the plate is lost, the first employee owns the $500 penalty — even though they no longer have the plate
- There is no way to assign accountability to the correct person
- The custody chain is broken

Peer-to-peer transfers destroy accountability. They are prohibited under all circumstances.

---

## 5. Accountability Agreement

### What It Is
A formal document that every employee must sign before they can access any key or dealer plate at the Edmonton Office.

### What It Covers
- Financial responsibility for lost keys, plates, and GPS tags
- Key Cafe compliance requirements (sign-out, sign-in, no peer-to-peer transfers)
- Custody chain requirements — "You signed it out" = your liability

### Consequence of Not Signing
An employee who has not signed the Accountability Agreement:
- Cannot take any dealer plate
- Cannot drive any dealership vehicle
- Cannot access Key Cafe for sign-out
- Cannot continue employment at the dealership

**There is no grace period. The agreement must be signed before any access is granted.**

---

## 6. GPS Key Tags

### What They Are
Every set of keys at the Edmonton Office has a GPS-enabled tag attached. The GPS tag enables physical tracking of the key set.

### Rules
- GPS key tags must remain attached to the key set at all times
- Tags must not be removed, damaged, or lost
- If a tag is separated from the key set: report it immediately

### Cost if Lost
**$25** — charged to the employee who lost the tag. This is an enforced penalty, not a theoretical one.

## 8. Enforcement Logic

| Scenario | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Employee signed a plate out | That employee owns liability for that plate until it is signed back in |
| Employee says "I gave it to Alex" | No defense — the signer still owns liability |
| Employee says "I don't know where it is" | Penalty applies to the last signer |
| Plate is found after penalty is paid | Penalty is not reversed — the loss of accountability happened |

**"You signed it out" is the complete and final determination of liability.** The Key Cafe log is the only evidence that matters.

## 9. Protocol Summary

| Situation | Correct Action |
|---|---|
| Need a key or plate | Go to Key Cafe. Sign it out under your name. |
| Done with a key or plate | Return to Key Cafe. Sign it back in immediately. |
| Another employee asks you for your plate | Say: "No — go back to Key Cafe, I'll check mine in, you sign it out." |
| Key or plate is missing | Report immediately. Last signer is accountable. |
| GPS tag is missing from a key set | Report immediately. |
| New employee needs key/plate access | Ensure Accountability Agreement is signed first. No exceptions. |
