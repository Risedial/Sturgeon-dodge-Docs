## Overview

The daily operations workflow establishes the rhythm of the Edmonton Office lot. Every operational day follows the same structure: arrive, handle immediate fires, execute the morning lot walk, generate and route the task list, and police the lot throughout the day.

---

## 1. Morning Lot Walk

### Status
**Mandatory.** The morning lot walk is not optional. It does not get skipped because the day is busy. Every operational issue discovered later in the day is harder to fix than one caught during the walk.

### Timing
- Arrive at work
- Handle immediate fires (urgent issues that cannot wait)
- Begin lot walk approximately 30 minutes after arriving
- Duration: approximately 30 minutes

### Who Conducts It
Lot Manager / Operations Manager (role confirmed: Scott [AMBIGUOUS]) or designee.

### What the Walk Covers
Every zone. Every vehicle. No zone is skipped.

Zones to walk, in order:
1. Cage (all 20 slots)
2. East Side Fence Line (all 5 slots + any Overflow)
3. West Side of Building (all 5 slots)
4. Overflow (Temporary) area
5. Power Sport / Quad Corner
6. Auction Area
7. Staff Parking (confirm no staff vehicles are on the lot)

### What to Check Per Vehicle

For every vehicle encountered during the walk:

| Check | Standard | Action if Failing |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle is in the correct zone for its status | Category matches zone (NEW/FLR → Cage; SOLD/BND → East Side Fence Line; RECON/BND overflow → West Side of Building) | Flag for repositioning — route to correct zone |
| Signage is present and correct | Stock-in tag in bottom-right windshield; sold sign if sold; trade-in banner if trade-in; customer sign if customer vehicle | Request correct sign placement immediately |
| Vehicle is clean and detailed | Acceptable display condition (no dirt, grime, visible defects that detailing would address) | Route to DHG for detail |
| Positioning is correct | Facing outward; adequate door-opening spacing | Reposition vehicle |
| Stock-in tag is present | White tag in bottom-right corner of windshield | Flag — route for tagging |
| No unauthorized personal vehicles on the lot | All vehicles are accounted for as inventory, sold, trade-in, BND, recon, or customer | Identify the vehicle (check staff vehicle inventory list); address with the owner |
| No compliance gaps | PDI complete if on front line; ship mode cleared if new arrival; no overdue PDI deadlines | Flag and route to service for immediate PDI |

### Output: Task List

The lot walk generates a task list. Every issue discovered during the walk becomes a task.

**Task routing:**
- PDI needed → route vehicle to service department, request priority processing
- Detailing needed → request DHG (external vendor) or quick wash through Hughes
- Sold sign missing → contact Sales Manager (Kevin) using 3-strike escalation protocol (see communication-and-escalation-protocols.md)
- Vehicle in wrong zone → move vehicle to correct zone
- Vehicle without stock-in tag → route vehicle to Admin — Stock Tags (Jorja) / Admin/Tech (Giselle) for tagging
- Staff vehicle on lot → address with the employee (see communication-and-escalation-protocols.md)
- PDI deadline at risk → alert service department immediately with vehicle info and deadline

**Documentation:** All issues from the morning lot walk must be documented in team chats. Documentation creates an accountability trail — if an issue recurs, there is a record of when it was first identified and what action was requested.

## 2. Staff Vehicle Inventory — One-Time Setup + Ongoing Maintenance

### Purpose
The lot team must be able to identify every vehicle on the lot at a glance. Without knowing which vehicles belong to staff, it is impossible to distinguish inventory from a staff member's personal car parked illegally on the lot.

### What Is Needed
A complete list of every staff member's personal vehicle, containing:
- Employee name
- Vehicle make
- Vehicle model
- Plate number

### Who Is Responsible
Manager — Lot Policing / Staff Vehicles (Don [AMBIGUOUS] / Alex [AMBIGUOUS]).

**Status at time of walkthrough:** Don / Alex reported that they had begun compiling this list on the morning of the walkthrough.

### How to Use It
During the morning lot walk, any vehicle that is not recognized as inventory, a sold vehicle, a trade-in, a customer vehicle, or an Overflow vehicle should be looked up against the staff vehicle inventory list. If it matches a staff vehicle, it is an illegal lot parking violation and should be addressed per the staff parking protocol.

### Maintenance
The staff vehicle inventory list must be updated whenever:
- A new employee joins the dealership
- An employee changes vehicles
- An employee leaves the dealership

## 3. Ongoing Lot Policing — Throughout the Day

The morning lot walk is not the only time standards are enforced. Lot staff must police the lot throughout the day for:

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| Staff vehicle parked on lot (not on street) | Address with employee using parking protocol (see communication-and-escalation-protocols.md) |
| Vehicle moved without authorization | Investigate who moved it and why; correct the placement |
| New arrival not processed | Immediately check STOCK HOLDER in Airtable; route through the correct arrival process |
| Sold vehicle without sign | Apply 3-strike escalation (see communication-and-escalation-protocols.md) |
| Vehicle without stock-in tag | Flag and route for tagging |
| Cage slot opened (vehicle sold or moved) | Check inventory — is there a vehicle ready to fill that slot? |

**Standard for policing:** "Inspect what you expect." Standards only hold through daily, consistent enforcement. Not one-time announcements.

## 4. Service Department Routing — Throughout the Day

Every vehicle that needs any work should be routed to the service department. This is both a compliance requirement (PDI must happen within 2 days) and a financial strategy (9 service staff at $3,000/day must be productively utilized).

### What to Route to Service
- New arrivals requiring PDI
- Dealer trades requiring final PDI
- Vehicles in need of mechanical inspection before retail sale
- Any vehicle flagged during the morning lot walk as needing mechanical attention

### How to Route
Lot Manager communicates directly to Service Department Lead [NEEDS_INPUT — contact person TBD; to be discussed]: vehicle stock number, VIN, what work is needed, and deadline (PDI: 2 days from arrival date).

### Priority Situations
If a vehicle's delivery is imminent and PDI is not yet complete: contact service department immediately with the delivery date and request priority processing. PDI still happens — the customer waits.
