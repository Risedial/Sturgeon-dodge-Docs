## Overview

The Edmonton Office service department has 9 staff and a daily labor cost of approximately $3,000. On the day of the walkthrough, the department served only 1 customer. This represents critical underutilization and a direct daily financial loss.

The solution — routing all internal vehicle work (PDIs, inspections, detailing coordination) to the service department — converts this underutilization into productive, justified overhead.

## 4. The Utilization Problem and Solution

### The Problem
With 9 staff at $3,000/day and only 1 customer on the day of the walkthrough, the service department is generating revenue equivalent to 1 customer while costing the equivalent of a much larger customer volume. The labor overhead is not being justified.

### The Solution
Route all internal vehicle work to the service department. This includes:
- PDI for every new vehicle arrival (required within 2 days by Stellantis)
- PDI for every dealer trade arrival
- All mechanical inspections before reconditioning
- Any mechanical work identified during the morning lot walk

### The Effect
Routing internal work to service converts the department from "1 customer" worth of productivity to "1 customer + all internal lot work." This:
- Justifies the $3,000/day labor cost
- Reduces the financial gap from underutilization
- Keeps technicians productive
- Accelerates PDI completion and improves Stellantis compliance

## 5. Relationship Between Service Department and Lot Operations

The service department is not just a compliance function — it is a financial asset that is currently being underused. Every vehicle that arrives on the lot and needs PDI is an opportunity to route billable internal work hours through service.

**Connection to compliance:** Every PDI routed to service and completed within 2 days improves Stellantis compliance. Currently at 80%, the compliance rate must improve to avoid manufacturer fines. Service department productivity directly drives compliance improvement.

**Connection to lot operations:** The lot team cannot place a vehicle in the Cage until service has completed PDI and the vehicle has been detailed. The service department is in the critical path for every new vehicle reaching the front line.
