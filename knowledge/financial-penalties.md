## Penalty Enforcement Principles

### "You Signed It Out"
The Key Cafe log is the definitive record. The last person to sign out a key or dealer plate owns financial liability for that item until it is signed back in. No alternative defense is accepted.

- "I gave it to someone else" — not a valid defense
- "I don't know where it is" — the penalty still applies
- "I only had it for a minute" — the penalty still applies

### Accountability Agreement
Every employee must sign the Accountability Agreement before receiving any key or plate access. The agreement formalizes the financial responsibility outlined above. Signing the agreement means the employee has acknowledged and accepted these penalty terms.
