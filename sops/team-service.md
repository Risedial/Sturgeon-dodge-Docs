## Section 1 — Your Role in Lot Operations

The service department performs PDIs and executes recon work for the Edmonton Office lot. This work is not optional overhead — it is the financial and compliance justification for the department's existence.

**The numbers:**
- 9 service staff at approximately $3,000/day in combined labor cost
- On the day of the lot walkthrough, the service department served 1 external customer
- 1 customer's revenue does not justify $3,000/day in labor

**The solution:** Every internal vehicle that arrives on the Edmonton Office lot and requires PDI is routed through this department. Every vehicle that needs recon work before retail sale goes through this department. Internal lot work converts the department from critically underutilized to productively utilized — and directly improves Stellantis PDI compliance simultaneously.

**The current compliance problem:** Stellantis PDI compliance is at **80%** — in the red zone where financial fines and manufacturer docking begin. Every PDI this department completes on time is a step toward recovery. Every PDI this department misses or delays makes the compliance problem worse and brings manufacturer penalties closer.

**Underutilization is a direct financial loss. Internal PDI is both a compliance requirement and a financial recovery mechanism.**

## Section 2 — PDI Execution

### Who Performs PDI

Service technicians perform PDIs. Eligible technicians include all service technicians and Giselle (Admin/Tech — Stock Tags + Service) when assigned.

PDI assignments are routed by the Service Department Lead.

### What PDI Involves

A full Pre-Delivery Inspection per Stellantis requirements. This is the manufacturer's required inspection — it is not an informal check-over or a visual scan.

### What "PDI Complete" Means

**PDI is complete only when the technician has marked it complete in the manufacturer system.**

PDI is NOT complete if:
- The technician did a visual inspection but did not log it in the manufacturer system
- The PDI was performed at the originating factory or by another dealer (the final PDI at this location must still occur and be logged)
- The salesperson says the vehicle is "good to go"
- Verbal confirmation was given to the lot manager without system entry

**The manufacturer system entry is the only valid record of PDI completion.** All other statements are informal and carry no compliance weight.

### The 2-Day Window — Non-Negotiable

**Every vehicle delivered to the Edmonton Office lot must have its final PDI marked complete in the manufacturer system within 2 calendar days of the vehicle's arrival at this dealership.**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Deadline | 2 calendar days from vehicle arrival date |
| Clock starts | The day the vehicle arrives at the Edmonton Office |
| Applies to | All new vehicle arrivals |
| Applies to | All dealer trades arriving at this location |
| Evidence required | Entry in manufacturer system by the technician |

**Current compliance: 80% — entering the red zone where Stellantis fines and docking begin.**

### PDI Monitoring — Day-Level Escalation Protocol

The 2-day window requires active monitoring:

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| Vehicle arrives (Day 0) | Lot team notifies service department via WhatsApp with stock number, VIN, and arrival date. Service Department Lead assigns the PDI to a technician. |
| End of Day 1 | If PDI has not been completed: Service Department Lead escalates within the service department to ensure PDI is completed on Day 2. |
| End of Day 2 | If PDI has not been completed: Service Department Lead escalates to Lot Manager and General Manager immediately. This is a compliance breach. Document it. |

Do not allow a vehicle to reach the end of Day 2 without PDI complete. At that point the compliance breach has already occurred.

## Section 3 — Ship Mode Vehicles

Some new vehicles arrive in ship mode — a factory transport state in which the battery is disconnected and the software is in a low-power transport configuration.

### What Must Happen Before PDI Begins

For a vehicle in ship mode, complete these steps in order before PDI begins:

1. **Battery reconnection** — physically reconnect the vehicle battery
2. **Software reset** — perform the manufacturer-specified software reset procedure
3. **PDI** — perform the full Pre-Delivery Inspection and mark complete in manufacturer system

All three steps must happen in this sequence. No step can be skipped. No step can be substituted.

**Important:** The 2-day PDI deadline still runs from the vehicle's original arrival date, not from the date ship mode was cleared. Ship mode does not extend the PDI window. If a vehicle arrives on Day 1 in ship mode, the PDI must still be complete by end of Day 2.

### The Ship Mode Delivery Failure — Confirmed Real Incident

**A vehicle was delivered to a customer at this dealership while still in ship mode.** The battery was disconnected. PDI had not been performed. The detail had not been completed.

- The car died the next day. The customer was stranded.
- The vehicle required a tow back to the dealership.
- Total cost: $275 (skipped detail rework) + tow cost + complete rework of the entire preparation process.
- The customer experienced a failed delivery and had to wait while the work was redone.

**Ship mode vehicles cannot be placed on the front line or delivered to a customer until all three steps are complete.** This is not a policy recommendation — it is the confirmed, real-world consequence of skipping the process.

## Section 4 — Dealer Trade PDI

Dealer trades — vehicles arriving from another dealership — require a final PDI at the Edmonton Office.

**The rule:** Even if the originating dealership certifies the vehicle as ready, inspected, or PDI'd, the Edmonton Office must perform its own final PDI. This is a Stellantis requirement.

The same 2-day window applies: PDI must be marked complete in the manufacturer system within 2 calendar days of the vehicle arriving at this location.

Do not accept verbal or paper confirmation from the originating dealer as a substitute for performing the PDI here.

## Section 5 — PDI Failure Routing

If PDI reveals mechanical issues that prevent the vehicle from being placed on the front line:

**Step 1:** Do not return the vehicle to the Cage.

**Step 2:** Update the vehicle's status in Airtable to IN RECON.

**Step 3:** Move the vehicle to the West Side of Building (L1–L5 slots).

**Step 4:** Assign a recon work order and complete the required mechanical work.

**Step 5:** Notify the Lot Manager via WhatsApp team chat immediately:

> "PDI revealed mechanical issues on [VIN / stock number]. Status updated to IN RECON. Vehicle moved to West Side of Building. Recon work order assigned."

**Step 6:** When recon work is complete and the vehicle is ready to re-enter the PDI process: notify the Lot Manager via WhatsApp team chat.

For the full PDI failure routing process:
→ SEE: vehicle-recon-routing.md

## Section 6 — Internal Vehicle Work Prioritization

All PDIs, mechanical inspections, and internal vehicle work for the Edmonton Office lot come through the service department. This work must be treated as a priority — not as an afterthought.

**Do not turn away internal vehicle work.** Internal PDIs and recon work are the justification for the service department's $3,000/day labor cost. Turning away internal work leaves technicians unproductive and the financial gap unjustified.

**Priority order for internal work:**
1. PDIs approaching their 2-day deadline (time-critical — do first)
2. PDIs with a full day remaining
3. Recon work (mechanical repair before retail sale)
4. Other internal lot work

When a PDI delivery is imminent (customer is waiting for the vehicle), the Lot Manager will request priority PDI processing. Honor that request — expedite the PDI. The customer waits. The PDI is not skipped.

## Section 7 — Communication with Lot Team

When PDI is marked complete in the manufacturer system, immediately notify the Lot Manager via WhatsApp team chat.

**Use this exact language:**

> "PDI complete for [VIN]. Ready for detail and tagging."

Do not use informal confirmation only. Do not tell the lot team verbally without also posting in the WhatsApp team chat. The WhatsApp message is the documentation record that the PDI is done and the vehicle can move to the next stage.

**Information to include in all service-to-lot communications:**
- Vehicle VIN or stock number
- What was done (PDI complete, ship mode cleared, recon complete, etc.)
- Next step or current vehicle status
- Any issues that affect placement or delivery (mechanical problems, parts on order, etc.)

## Section 8 — Detailing Coordination

After PDI is complete, the vehicle must be detailed before it can be placed in the Cage. The service department coordinates with DHD (the primary external detailing vendor) for this step.

### What to Do After PDI Is Complete

1. Notify the Lot Manager via WhatsApp: *"PDI complete for [VIN]. Ready for detail and tagging."*
2. The Lot Manager routes the vehicle to DHD for full detail ($60 per vehicle)
3. When the vehicle returns from DHD and the Lot Team confirms the detail is complete to standard: the vehicle receives its stock-in tag and routes to the Cage

The service department's responsibility in detailing coordination ends at step 1. The lot team handles the DHD routing and vehicle return inspection.
