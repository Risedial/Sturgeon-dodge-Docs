## Section 1 — Your Role

### What the Lot Team Does

The lot team is responsible for the physical management and condition of every vehicle on the Edmonton Office lot. Your job is to ensure that every vehicle is in the correct zone, properly identified, clean, and compliant at all times.

**You are responsible for:**
- Executing the morning lot walk and generating the daily task list
- Processing vehicle arrivals (checking ship mode, routing to PDI, placing tags)
- Moving vehicles to the correct zone based on their status
- Placing all physical signage: sold signs, trade-in banners, customer vehicle signs, stock-in tags
- Routing vehicles to detailing (DHD) after PDI is complete
- Signing keys and dealer plates in and out through Key Cafe — never through a coworker
- Documenting all findings and tasks in the WhatsApp team chat
- Escalating issues that cannot be resolved at your level

### What the Lot Team Does NOT Do

**You do not:**
- Perform PDIs — that is the service department's job
- Make sales decisions or negotiate pricing — that is the sales team's job
- Determine whether a trade-in is auction-bound or retail — that is a management/sales decision
- Assign or change vehicle status in Airtable — report the issue and request the appropriate person make the change
- Move any vehicle without first confirming its status with the assigned salesperson

## Section 2 — Morning Lot Walk

The morning lot walk is **mandatory every operational day**. It does not get skipped because the day is busy.

**Timing:**
- Arrive at work
- Handle immediate fires (urgent issues that cannot wait — address within minutes)
- Begin the lot walk approximately 30 minutes after arriving
- Duration: approximately 30 minutes

**For the full decision-by-decision process for the morning lot walk:**

→ SEE: morning-lot-walk.md

**What to check on every vehicle during the walk:**

| Check | Standard | Action if Failing |
|---|---|---|
| Correct zone for status | NEW/FLR → Cage; SOLD/BND → East Side Fence Line; RECON/BND overflow → West Side of Building | Flag for repositioning — move to correct zone |
| Signage present and correct | Stock-in tag (white, bottom-right windshield); sold sign if sold; trade-in banner if trade-in; customer sign if customer vehicle | Request placement immediately |
| Vehicle is clean | No dirt, grime, or visible defects that detailing would address | Route to DHD for full detail |
| Correct positioning | Facing outward; adequate door-opening spacing | Reposition vehicle |
| No unauthorized vehicles | All vehicles accounted for as inventory, sold, trade-in, BND, recon, or customer | Cross-check against staff vehicle inventory list; if staff vehicle, address with employee |
| PDI compliance | No vehicle on the front line without completed PDI | Flag and route to service immediately |

**Generating the Task List:**

After completing the walk, compile every issue into a task list and document it in the WhatsApp team chat. Route each task:
- PDI needed → post in WhatsApp: vehicle stock number, VIN, PDI deadline; route vehicle to service department
- Detailing needed → route vehicle to DHD
- Sold sign missing → begin 3-strike escalation with Sales Manager (Kevin) → SEE: sold-sign-missing-enforcement.md
- Vehicle in wrong zone → move vehicle to correct zone
- Vehicle without stock-in tag → bring to Admin — Stock Tags (Jorja) or Admin/Tech — Stock Tags + Service (Giselle) for immediate tagging
- Staff vehicle on lot → address with employee per parking protocol
- PDI deadline at risk → alert service department lead immediately

**Post all findings and task assignments in the WhatsApp team chat.** Do not document only by memory or in-person conversation.

## Section 3 — Vehicle Arrival Processing

When a vehicle arrives on the lot, do not park it and walk away. Every arrival must be processed immediately.

**Step 1 — Check STOCK HOLDER in Airtable before anything else:**

| STOCK HOLDER value | Action |
|---|---|
| `STURGEON DODGE` | Proceed with arrival processing below |
| `NON PRIME DIVISION` | Notify General Manager immediately. Do NOT place vehicle anywhere on this lot. → SEE: vehicle-non-prime-identification.md |

**Step 2 — Check for ship mode:**

A vehicle is in ship mode if: the battery is disconnected, it will not start normally, or the service department identifies it as such.

| Ship mode? | Action |
|---|---|
| YES | Do not attempt to move or place the vehicle. Notify service department immediately. → SEE: vehicle-arrival-ship-mode.md |
| NO, standard new arrival | → SEE: vehicle-arrival-new-standard.md |
| NO, dealer trade | → SEE: vehicle-arrival-dealer-trade.md |

**What you do while the vehicle awaits PDI:**
- Keep the vehicle staged away from the Cage — it does not belong in the Cage until PDI is complete, it is fully detailed, and the stock-in tag is placed
- Do not assign a Cage slot to any vehicle that has not met all front-line-ready conditions
- Post the vehicle arrival in the WhatsApp team chat with stock number, VIN, and the date it arrived (needed to track the 2-day PDI deadline)

## Section 4 — Stock-In Tags

Every vehicle on the lot must have a white stock-in tag placed in the bottom-right corner of the windshield. No exceptions.

### Who Creates the Tags

- Admin — Stock Tags (Jorja) fills out stock-in tags and hands them to the lot team
- Admin/Tech — Stock Tags + Service (Giselle) also fills out stock-in tags and hands them to the lot team

**You do not fill out the tags yourself.** You receive completed tags from Jorja or Giselle and place them on the vehicles.

### When to Place

Place the stock-in tag on a vehicle **after PDI is complete and after detailing is complete** — as the vehicle moves toward front-line placement.

Do not place a stock-in tag on a vehicle that is still in PDI or still at the detailer. The tag signals the vehicle is processed and ready.

### Where to Place

**Bottom-right corner of the windshield** — always, every vehicle, no exceptions.

### Color

**White only.** Never use yellow tags. Yellow tags look like AutoWorld tags and cause confusion about vehicle origin and status.

### The Rule

Any vehicle on the lot without a stock-in tag has not been formally processed. If you find a vehicle without a tag during the morning lot walk: flag it immediately, bring it to Jorja or Giselle for tagging before any other action, and document the flag in the WhatsApp team chat.

## Section 5 — Vehicle Movement and Placement

### Before Moving Any Vehicle

**Do not move any vehicle until you have confirmed its status.** Ask the assigned salesperson:

> "Whose is this? What's the status? When is delivery?"

Based on their answer:
- **Delivery is imminent:** Expedite through PDI immediately — contact service department with stock number, VIN, and delivery date. Request priority PDI. Do not skip PDI under any circumstances. The customer waits.
- **Salesperson says "don't worry about it, it's good to go":** Respond: *"Stellantis requires final PDI within 2 days. We're at 80% compliance, in the red zone. It needs to go through."* If pushback continues, escalate to Lot Manager.
- **Vehicle is not ready for delivery:** Process normally through PDI → detail → placement. No urgency.

### Zone Assignment by Vehicle Status

| Vehicle Category | Correct Zone |
|---|---|
| NEW (KM ≤ 1,000) — PDI complete, fully detailed, tagged | Cage |
| FLR (AVAILABLE or DEMO) — PDI complete, fully detailed, tagged | Cage |
| SOLD (SIGNED DEAL or WHOLESALE \| SOLD) | East Side Fence Line (F1–F5); overflow → West Side of Building |
| BND (BOOKED \| NOT DELIVERED) | East Side Fence Line (F1–F5); overflow → West Side of Building |
| RECON (IN RECON, INCOMING, WHOLESALE, CHASE) | West Side of Building (L1–L5) |
| Overflow (when all fixed slots full) | Overflow (Temporary) — label vehicle with destination zone |

**CRITICAL:** New arrivals are NEVER placed in the West Side of Building with a RECON status. New arrivals are "Pending PDI." If someone has applied a RECON status to a new arrival, report it to the Lot Manager immediately — this hides the vehicle from the sales inventory system.

### Cage Placement Rules

For detailed Cage slot assignment rules (C01–C20 priority, brand priority, body type priority):

→ SEE: vehicle-placement-cage.md

**Key Cage rules you must apply every time:**
- All 20 Cage stalls must be full at all times. Maximum 1 empty stall permitted, and only if that slot's vehicle was just sold.
- All vehicles in the Cage must face outward toward customers.
- All vehicles must have adequate door-opening spacing — a customer must be able to fully open a door to enter and exit.
- No vehicle enters the Cage without: PDI complete + fully detailed + no stickers + no tape + stock-in tag placed.

## Section 6 — Signage Duties

Every vehicle on the lot must have correct identification signage. Without a sign, no one can tell what that vehicle is.

**THE KIA INCIDENT:** A Kia sat on the Edmonton Office lot for 6 weeks with no identification signage. No one knew whether it was inventory, a sold vehicle, a trade-in, or a customer car. No one moved it, processed it, or took action. For 6 weeks, the vehicle was completely paralyzed. This is what happens without proper signage. Every vehicle you encounter must have its sign.

### Sold Signs

**When:** Place a sold sign the moment a vehicle is sold. Do not wait until the end of the day. Do not wait to be asked a second time. The sign goes in immediately.

**Who requests it:** The sales team is responsible for placing sold signs. When the Sales Manager (Kevin) or a salesperson requests the lot team to place a sold sign, do it immediately.

**What to do:**
1. Get a sold sign from upstairs (sold sign supplies are kept upstairs with Sharpies)
2. Write the customer's name on the sign with a Sharpie
3. Place the sign visibly inside the vehicle window

**If you discover a sold vehicle without a sold sign during the morning lot walk:**
→ SEE: sold-sign-missing-enforcement.md

### Trade-In Banners

**When:** Place a trade-in banner immediately when a customer delivers a trade-in to the lot.

**Who requests it:** The sales team notifies the lot team via WhatsApp team chat when a trade-in is arriving with the vehicle description and initial condition assessment. On receipt of that notification — or on physical receipt of the vehicle — place the banner immediately.

**Supplies:** Upstairs, same location as sold sign supplies.

**Important:** Trade-in vehicles do NOT go in the Cage. A trade-in has not been through PDI, detail, or processing. It goes to the appropriate zone based on its condition (as determined by sales/management), and the lot team places the banner to identify it.

### Customer Vehicle Signs

**When:** Place a customer vehicle sign immediately when a customer's personal vehicle arrives on the lot for any reason (service drop-off, waiting, etc.).

**What to write:** *"Customer's car, picking up [date]"* — include the expected pickup date.

**Where:** On or in the vehicle, visibly placed so any lot staff member can read it at a glance.

For the full customer vehicle handling process:
→ SEE: vehicle-customer-on-lot.md

## Section 7 — Key Cafe Responsibilities

**Every key and every dealer plate at the Edmonton Office must go through Key Cafe. There are no exceptions, no shortcuts, and no workarounds.**

### Location

Key Cafe is located on the 3rd floor by Jorja's desk.

### Sign-Out Process

1. Go to Key Cafe (3rd floor by Jorja's desk)
2. Sign out the key or plate under your name
3. Your name and the timestamp are logged
4. Take the item

**No one takes a key or plate without a logged sign-out entry. No exceptions.**

### Sign-In Process

1. Return to Key Cafe immediately when you are done with the key or plate
2. Sign the item back in under your name
3. Your name and the timestamp are logged

**"Immediately" means when you are done — not at the end of the day, not when it is convenient.**

### No Peer-to-Peer Transfers — ABSOLUTE RULE

**Never accept a key or dealer plate directly from a coworker.**

**Never hand a key or dealer plate directly to a coworker.**

If a coworker asks you for the plate or key you currently have signed out, say exactly:

> **"No — go back to Key Cafe, I'll check mine in, you sign it out."**

This is the exact language. There is no alternative acceptable response.

**Why this rule is non-negotiable:** If you hand a plate to a coworker without going through Key Cafe, Key Cafe still shows you as responsible. If that plate is lost, you pay the $500 penalty — even though you no longer have it. The custody chain exists to protect you.

### Financial Penalties (All Enforced)

| Item | Penalty | Who Pays |
|---|---|---|
| Dealer plate (lost) | **$500** | Employee who last signed it out |
| GPS key tag (lost) | **$25** | Employee who lost it |
| Vehicle keys (lost) | **~50% of replacement cost** | Employee who lost them |

The $500 dealer plate penalty has a confirmed precedent — an employee paid it. It was not forgiven.

**Dealer plates and keys are the equivalent of carrying $500 cash. Treat them accordingly.**

### Before Your First Key/Plate Access

You must sign the Accountability Agreement before you can sign out any key or dealer plate. If you have not signed it, go to management immediately. Without the signed agreement, you cannot access Key Cafe.

## Section 8 — Detailing Coordination

After PDI is complete, the vehicle must be fully detailed before it can go to the Cage.

### The Standard Process

1. Service department notifies you via WhatsApp: *"PDI complete for [VIN]. Ready for detail and tagging."*
2. Route the vehicle to DHD (primary external detailing vendor) for a full detail at $60 per vehicle
3. DHD completes the detail and returns the vehicle to the lot
4. When the vehicle returns from DHD, inspect it:
   - Are all stickers removed? (Must be yes)
   - Are all tape residues removed? (Must be yes)
   - Is the vehicle clean to display standard? (Must be yes)
5. If the detail meets standard: obtain the stock-in tag from Jorja or Giselle, place it in the bottom-right corner of the windshield, then route the vehicle to the Cage

**If the detail is substandard (stickers still on, vehicle not clean enough for front-line display):**
- Do NOT accept the vehicle as complete
- Document the deficiency in the WhatsApp team chat
- Return the vehicle to DHD with specific instruction on what needs to be redone

For the full detailing routing decision process:
→ SEE: vehicle-detailing-routing.md

### Quick Wash (Hughes)

Hughes is used for a quick wash-through when a vehicle has been detailed or is brand new but has a lightly soiled exterior. Hughes is NOT a substitute for a full detail when a full detail is required. A Hughes wash alone does not qualify a vehicle for front-line placement if the vehicle truly requires a full detail.

## Section 9 — Reporting Issues

Every issue you find — whether during the morning lot walk or during the day — must be documented in the WhatsApp team chat.

**Documentation is not optional.** If you report an issue verbally but not in the team chat, there is no record. If the issue recurs, there is no evidence it was ever raised.

### Issue Routing

| Issue Found | What to Do |
|---|---|
| Sold vehicle without sold sign | Begin 3-strike escalation → SEE: sold-sign-missing-enforcement.md |
| Vehicle with no status signage or unknown identity | → SEE: vehicle-status-unknown.md |
| Vehicle in wrong zone | Move to correct zone; document in team chat |
| New arrival not yet processed | Check STOCK HOLDER in Airtable; route through correct arrival process |
| Staff vehicle parked on lot | Address with employee per parking protocol; document in team chat |
| Vehicle without stock-in tag | Route to Jorja or Giselle for tagging; document in team chat |
| PDI deadline at risk | Post in WhatsApp immediately with stock number, VIN, arrival date, deadline date, and urgency. Route to Service Department Lead immediately. |
| Non-prime vehicle identified | Notify General Manager immediately. Do not place vehicle anywhere on the lot. → SEE: vehicle-non-prime-identification.md |

### Escalation Chain

Lot Attendant → Lot Manager → Sales Manager (Kevin) → General Manager

When you have used the defined protocol for an issue and it has not resolved, escalate to the Lot Manager. Do not attempt a fourth strike, do not improvise, and do not let the issue sit unreported.
