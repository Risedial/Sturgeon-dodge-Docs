## Section 1 — Enforcement Philosophy

**Standards only hold through daily, consistent enforcement.**

A single announcement does not create a standard. An email does not create a standard. A walkthrough does not create a standard. Only daily, visible, consistent enforcement creates a standard.

**"Inspect what you expect."** If management is not physically present and checking that standards are met, they are not being met. The lot, signage, PDI compliance, and Key Cafe adherence all degrade the moment management stops actively enforcing them.

**Management must be present and visible in enforcement.** This means:
- Walking the lot personally
- Using the defined escalation language directly with the relevant party
- Following through on financial penalties without making exceptions
- Addressing violations immediately — not at the next meeting, not next week

**One exception destroys the standard.** If management allows one staff vehicle on the lot, the standard becomes "sometimes you can park on the lot." If management waives a penalty once, the standard becomes "penalties are negotiable." Do not make exceptions.

## Section 2 — Daily Oversight Responsibilities

### Lot Manager

**Mandatory daily:**

1. **Execute the morning lot walk** within 30 minutes of arriving. Duration: approximately 30 minutes. Every zone, every vehicle.
   → SEE: morning-lot-walk.md

2. **Generate and distribute the task list.** Every issue found during the walk becomes a task. Post the task list in the WhatsApp team chat with routing instructions (PDI → service; detailing → DHD; sold sign → 3-strike escalation; wrong zone → lot team).

3. **Police the lot throughout the day.** Standards are not enforced once in the morning and then left alone. Ongoing policing includes:
   - Staff vehicles on the lot → address immediately
   - New arrivals not processed → route through correct arrival process
   - Sold vehicles without signs → initiate 3-strike escalation
   - Vehicles in wrong zones → route for repositioning
   - PDI deadlines approaching → alert service department lead immediately

4. **Monitor PDI deadlines.** Know which vehicles are approaching their 2-day window. Escalate before the deadline passes — not after.

   | Timing | Action |
   |---|---|
   | Vehicle arrives (Day 0) | Post in WhatsApp: stock number, VIN, arrival date. Notify service department. |
   | End of Day 1 — PDI not complete | Escalate directly to Service Department Lead: "PDI for [VIN] needs to be complete tomorrow. Day 2 deadline." |
   | End of Day 2 — PDI not complete | Escalate to General Manager immediately. Document the incident. |

5. **Route all service department communications in writing.** Every instruction to the service department (PDI request, urgency flag, recon work order) is posted in WhatsApp in addition to verbal communication.

### Sales Manager (Kevin)

**Daily responsibilities:**

1. **Ensure sold signs are placed immediately** when any vehicle is sold. This is Kevin's direct accountability — not the lot team's sole responsibility.

2. **Ensure the sales team communicates delivery dates** to the lot team via WhatsApp immediately when a deal is signed. If this is not happening, address it directly with the relevant sales staff.

3. **Address staff parking violations from sales staff directly.** Sales staff park on the street. Kevin parks on the street. No exceptions. When a sales team member parks on the lot, Kevin addresses it personally — the Lot Manager should not be the only enforcer.

4. **Handle Strike 3 escalations for sold sign compliance.** When the Lot Manager has used the 3-strike protocol and the issue remains unresolved at Strike 3, Kevin intervenes directly and places the sign himself or personally ensures it is placed immediately.

5. **Never direct sales staff to skip or bypass PDI.** If a customer is requesting immediate delivery before PDI is complete, the sales team's response is to expedite the PDI — not to skip it. Kevin is accountable for his team's PDI cooperation.

### General Manager

**Daily responsibilities:**

1. **Final escalation point for all unresolved compliance failures.** When Lot Manager and Sales Manager have both addressed an issue and it remains unresolved, General Manager intervenes directly.

2. **Review compliance issues at the management level.** Monitor PDI compliance rate trend. At 80% compliance (current status), active recovery is required — not passive monitoring.

3. **Enforce financial penalties** per the financial accountability section below.

4. **Non-prime vehicle response.** If any non-prime vehicle is identified on the lot, the General Manager is notified immediately and takes personal responsibility for routing it to AB | STURGEON DODGE without delay.

## Section 3 — Staff Vehicle Inventory

### What It Is

A complete list of every staff member's personal vehicle, maintained by management. This list is the tool that enables the lot team to identify which vehicles on the lot belong to staff (and should not be there) versus inventory, sold vehicles, trade-ins, or customer vehicles.

### Required Fields

For each staff member:
- Employee name
- Vehicle make
- Vehicle model
- Plate number

### Who Compiles and Maintains It

Management is responsible. Assign a specific person at management level to own this list. It does not maintain itself.

### When to Update

- When a new employee joins: add their vehicle on Day 1 (or when they start driving their vehicle to work)
- When an employee changes vehicles: update immediately
- When an employee leaves: remove them
- Minimum: review the list monthly and confirm it is current

### How It Is Used

During the morning lot walk, the Lot Manager uses the staff vehicle inventory list to identify any vehicle on the lot that is not recognized as inventory, sold, trade-in, BND, recon, or customer. If that vehicle matches a staff entry on the list: it is an illegal parking violation. Address it per the staff parking protocol.

## Section 5 — PDI Compliance Ownership

**Management owns recovery from 80% compliance to 100% compliance.**

At 80%, the Edmonton Office is in the red zone — Stellantis fines and docking begin at or near this compliance level. This is not a future risk. It is the current state.

Recovery requires:
- Every new vehicle arrival being routed to service for PDI within the same day it arrives
- Every PDI being completed by the service department within the 2-day window
- Active monitoring by the Lot Manager of every vehicle's PDI status and deadline
- Escalation at the management level when PDI is not completed on time

### Management Escalation Protocol for PDI

| Situation | Action | Who Acts |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle arrives (Day 0) | Lot Manager routes to service; documents in WhatsApp | Lot Manager |
| End of Day 1 — PDI not complete | Lot Manager escalates directly to Service Department Lead: "PDI for [VIN] needs to be complete tomorrow. Day 2 deadline." | Lot Manager |
| End of Day 2 — PDI not complete | Lot Manager escalates to General Manager immediately. General Manager escalates to Service Department Lead directly and documents the incident. | Lot Manager → General Manager |
| Compliance rate drops below 80% | General Manager engages Stellantis contact; initiates service department productivity review | General Manager |

**Do not wait for a Day 2 breach to act.** Identify at-risk vehicles at end of Day 1 and resolve before the deadline.

## Section 6 — 3-Strike Escalation Management

Management is both the enforcer (uses 3-strike language to push compliance with the team) and the escalation recipient (when a Lot Attendant has used all 3 strikes without resolution).

### When Lot Team Escalates a Strike 3 to Management

When a Lot Attendant has used the 3-strike protocol for a missing sold sign (or similar compliance issue) and the issue remains unresolved at Strike 3, the escalation comes to the Sales Manager (Kevin) or the General Manager.

**At Strike 3 escalation from the lot team: act immediately.**

Do not wait for a fourth strike. Do not schedule a meeting for later. The moment escalation arrives at management, management intervenes directly.

### How Management Intervenes at Strike 3

**Sales Manager (Kevin) receives Strike 3 escalation for sold sign:**
- Kevin goes to the vehicle personally
- Kevin places the sign himself or directly instructs the relevant salesperson to do it now — not later
- Kevin documents in WhatsApp that the sign has been placed

**General Manager receives any Strike 3 escalation:**
- General Manager addresses the issue directly and immediately
- If the issue involves a compliance pattern (not a one-time miss), the General Manager initiates a formal conversation with the responsible party about the repeated failure

### 3-Strike Language Reference

The exact language of the 3-strike protocol (for sold sign enforcement):

| Strike | Language |
|---|---|
| Strike 1 (Lot Manager to Kevin) | "Hey, Kevin, do you mind getting your guys to put a sold sign in there?" |
| Strike 2 (Lot Manager to Kevin) | "Hey, Kevin, do you mind?" |
| Strike 3 (Lot Manager to Kevin) | "Kev, killing me, buddy. Can you just tell me the stuff and I'll do it, because we have to be organized." |
| Expected response at Strike 3 | "No, no, buddy, I'm coming down myself. I'll do it myself." |

## Section 8 — New Employee Onboarding Protocol

Every new employee who will interact with keys, plates, or lot operations must complete the following on Day 1 — before they begin work.

### Day 1 Checklist (Management's Responsibility to Ensure Completion)

1. **Accountability Agreement signed** — before any key or plate access. The signed document goes into the accountability agreement file on the same day.
   → SEE: accountability-agreement-onboarding.md

2. **Key Cafe training completed** — the employee is shown Key Cafe (3rd floor by Jorja's desk), shown how to sign out and sign in, and told the no peer-to-peer transfer rule explicitly.

3. **Parking rules explained** — with a welcoming and non-confrontational tone:

   → SEE: staff-parking-new-employee.md

   **Use this language:**
   > "Hey, welcome aboard. All sales have to park on the street — limited space, landlord parking, big trucks coming through."

   Frame it as standard practice, not as a personal restriction. The goal is that the employee understands and complies without feeling targeted.

4. **Staff vehicle added to the staff vehicle inventory list** — employee's name, make, model, and plate recorded in the management list on Day 1.

### Why Day 1 Is Non-Negotiable

The confirmed incident at this dealership: an employee (Chris's son — a new employee trying to get into the industry) parked on the lot because nobody told him the rules before he arrived. The correct response was to inform him kindly. The root cause was that management did not establish the expectations before the employee's first day.

**Onboarding protocol prevents this.** When expectations are set on Day 1, first-day compliance failures do not occur.

## Section 9 — Key Cafe Oversight

Management is responsible for the integrity of the Key Cafe system at the organizational level.

### Ongoing Management Responsibilities

1. **Maintain the signed Accountability Agreement file.** Every current employee must have a signed agreement on file. Review this file quarterly. If any employee is missing a signed agreement, remediate immediately — no key or plate access until the agreement is signed.

2. **Review Key Cafe logs when any item is lost or reported missing.** The log identifies the last signer. The last signer is accountable. Identify the party, apply the penalty, and document the resolution.

3. **Audit Key Cafe logs periodically for peer-to-peer transfer patterns.** Look for: item signed out by Employee A and signed back in by Employee B (without Employee B having a separate sign-out entry). This indicates a peer-to-peer transfer occurred. Address the pattern with both employees immediately.

4. **Confirm all employees know the no peer-to-peer rule.** The rule is: the last signer is responsible until the item is signed back in at Key Cafe. There is no defense. Ensure this is understood at hiring and reinforced through periodic reminders.

### Key Cafe Location

Key Cafe is on the 3rd floor by Jorja's desk.
