# Vehicle Audit Checklist — SOLD
**Category:** SOLD — Airtable status SIGNED DEAL or WHOLESALE|SOLD
**Used by:** Lot Attendant or Lot Manager during morning walk
**Status:** [COMPLETE]

---

## Purpose

This checklist verifies that a SOLD vehicle is correctly identified, correctly parked, and that PDI is on track before delivery. All items are binary pass/fail. Every No has a Failure Action with a named responsible role.

---

## Checklist Items

---

[ ] **1. Is a sold sign present in the vehicle with the customer's name written on it?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → initiate the 3-strike escalation protocol directed at Sales Manager (Kevin) → SEE: sold-sign-missing-enforcement.md:
    - Strike 1: SAY to Kevin: "Hey, Kevin, do you mind getting your guys to put a sold sign in there?"
    - Strike 2 (if not resolved after Strike 1): SAY to Kevin: "Hey, Kevin, do you mind?"
    - Strike 3 (if not resolved after Strike 2): SAY to Kevin: "Kev, killing me, buddy. Can you just tell me the stuff and I'll do it, because we have to be organized."
    If salesperson cannot be reached at any strike level and the sign remains missing: ROLE: Lot Manager → obtain customer name from Sales Manager (Kevin); get sold sign from upstairs (Sharpies available at same location); write customer name on sign; place sign visibly inside the vehicle. Document the missing sign and corrective action in WhatsApp team chat.

---

[ ] **2. Is the vehicle parked in the East Side Fence Line (F1–F5) or West Side of Building (L1–L5) — NOT in the Cage or any front-line zone?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → move vehicle out of the Cage or incorrect zone immediately; primary destination is East Side Fence Line (slots F1–F5); if East Side Fence Line is full: move to West Side of Building (slots L1–L5); if both zones are full: move to Overflow (Temporary) and annotate destination in Airtable and WhatsApp team chat. Document the move in WhatsApp.

---

[ ] **3. Is PDI confirmed complete in the manufacturer system before the vehicle is delivered to the customer?**
    YES (or delivery is not yet scheduled — no action required): Continue
    NO AND delivery is imminent (customer has a confirmed delivery appointment or is on their way) — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → do NOT allow delivery to proceed; contact Service Department Lead immediately via WhatsApp: "PDI for [VIN] has not been marked complete in the manufacturer system. Delivery cannot proceed. Stellantis requires PDI completion within 2 days of vehicle arrival. Please prioritize PDI now." Escalate to General Manager immediately if PDI cannot be expedited before the customer arrives → SEE: vehicle-pdi-compliance-deadline.md. Document the situation in WhatsApp.

---

## Pass Criteria

Vehicle passes this checklist only when all applicable items are answered YES. Item 3 is conditional — if no delivery is imminent, it is a monitoring item only. A SOLD vehicle with a missing sold sign (Item 1 NO) or incorrect parking (Item 2 NO) requires immediate corrective action regardless of delivery status.
