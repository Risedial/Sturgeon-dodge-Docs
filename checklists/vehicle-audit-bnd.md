# Vehicle Audit Checklist — BND
**Category:** BND (Booked Not Delivered) — Airtable status BOOKED|NOT DELIVERED
**Used by:** Lot Attendant or Lot Manager during morning walk
**Status:** [COMPLETE]

---

## Purpose

This checklist verifies that a Booked Not Delivered vehicle is correctly identified, correctly parked, has a documented delivery date, and that PDI status is confirmed before delivery. All items are binary pass/fail. Every No has a Failure Action with a named responsible role.

**Definition:** A BND vehicle has an active deal signed on paper but the customer has not yet physically taken possession of the vehicle. It is parked in the East Side Fence Line or West Side of Building until delivery occurs.

---

## Checklist Items

---

[ ] **1. Is a sold sign present in the vehicle with the customer's name written on it?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → initiate the 3-strike escalation protocol directed at Sales Manager (Kevin) → SEE: sold-sign-missing-enforcement.md:
    - Strike 1: SAY to Kevin: "Hey, Kevin, do you mind getting your guys to put a sold sign in there?"
    - Strike 2 (if not resolved after Strike 1): SAY to Kevin: "Hey, Kevin, do you mind?"
    - Strike 3 (if not resolved after Strike 2): SAY to Kevin: "Kev, killing me, buddy. Can you just tell me the stuff and I'll do it, because we have to be organized."
    If salesperson cannot be reached at any strike level and the sign remains missing: ROLE: Lot Manager → obtain customer name from Sales Manager (Kevin); get sold sign from upstairs (Sharpies available at same location); write customer name on sign; place sign visibly inside the vehicle. Document the missing sign and corrective action in WhatsApp team chat.

---

[ ] **2. Is the vehicle parked in the East Side Fence Line (F1–F5) or West Side of Building (L1–L5) — NOT in the Cage?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → move vehicle out of the Cage or any front-line zone immediately; primary destination is East Side Fence Line (slots F1–F5); if East Side Fence Line is full: move to West Side of Building (slots L1–L5); if both are full: move to Overflow (Temporary) and annotate destination in Airtable and WhatsApp. Document the move in WhatsApp team chat.

---

[ ] **3. Is the expected delivery date for this vehicle documented in the WhatsApp team chat?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → contact Sales Manager (Kevin) via WhatsApp team chat immediately. SAY: "What is the expected delivery date for [VIN / customer name]? We need this documented in team chat so the lot team can manage the space and track PDI timing."

---

[ ] **4. Is PDI confirmed complete in the manufacturer system?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → route to Service Department Lead via WhatsApp immediately: "PDI not yet confirmed complete in manufacturer system for BND vehicle [VIN]. Stellantis 2-day window from arrival date [date]. Please assign and prioritize." Do not deliver the vehicle to the customer until PDI is marked complete in the manufacturer system; document the PDI status and any outstanding deadline risk in WhatsApp team chat.

---

## Pass Criteria

Vehicle passes this checklist only when all 4 items are answered YES. A BND vehicle with a missing sold sign, incorrect parking, missing delivery date documentation, or incomplete PDI requires immediate corrective action per the Failure Actions above.

---

## Notes

- Sold sign supplies are kept upstairs in the dealership building. Sharpies are kept with the sold signs.
- `[NEEDS_INPUT]`: Formal escalation consequence if Strike 3 of the 3-strike sold sign protocol fails to produce the sold sign — not confirmed in source material.
- PDI compliance: The 2-day Stellantis window runs from the vehicle's arrival at the Edmonton Office — not from the date the deal was signed. A BND vehicle that has been on the lot for some time may still have a PDI deadline outstanding if it arrived recently.
- Item 3 (delivery date documentation): This information enables the lot team to anticipate when the slot will open, plan PDI timing, and ensure the vehicle is ready for delivery on the confirmed date. Without it, the team cannot manage the lot or PDI schedule effectively.
