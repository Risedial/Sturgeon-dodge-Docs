# Vehicle Audit Checklist — RECON
**Category:** RECON
**Airtable Status:** IN RECON, INCOMING, WHOLESALE, or CHASE
**Used by:** Lot Attendant or Lot Manager during morning walk
**Zone:** West Side of Building (L1–L5)

---

## Instructions

Complete this checklist for every vehicle currently classified as RECON on the lot. Every item is answered Yes or No only. Every No triggers the specified Failure Action immediately — do not defer.

---

## Checklist

[ ] **1. Is the Airtable system status correct for a RECON vehicle — one of: IN RECON, INCOMING, WHOLESALE, or CHASE — and NOT "Pending PDI" unless it is a new arrival whose PDI revealed mechanical issues?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → correct Airtable status immediately; if uncertain what status to apply → SEE: vehicle-categorization.md; document correction in WhatsApp team chat

[ ] **2. Is the vehicle parked in the West Side of Building (slots L1–L5)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → move vehicle to West Side of Building immediately; document move in WhatsApp team chat

[ ] **3. Is a stock-in tag (white) present in the bottom-right corner of the vehicle windshield?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → obtain stock-in tag from Admin — Stock Tags (Jorja) or Admin/Tech — Stock Tags + Service (Giselle); place tag in bottom-right corner of windshield; document in WhatsApp team chat

[ ] **4. Is there an active work order or recon task assigned to this vehicle in the service department?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → contact Service Department Lead immediately via WhatsApp team chat; SAY: "No work order assigned for RECON vehicle [VIN]. Please assign a work order immediately."

[ ] **5. If RECON work is complete: is PDI also confirmed complete in the manufacturer system (not verbal — check system)?**
    YES (PDI confirmed complete in manufacturer system): Continue
    YES (RECON is not yet complete — this check does not apply yet): Continue
    NO and RECON is complete — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → contact Service Department Lead immediately via WhatsApp team chat; SAY: "RECON is complete for [VIN] but PDI is not yet marked complete in the manufacturer system. Route to PDI immediately — do not move vehicle to Cage until PDI is complete."; vehicle stays in West Side of Building until PDI is marked complete in system

[ ] **6. If both RECON and PDI are complete: is full detail (DHD, $60) also confirmed complete before moving to the Cage?**
    YES (detail confirmed complete): Continue — vehicle may be moved to Cage
    YES (RECON and/or PDI not yet complete — this check does not apply yet): Continue
    NO and both RECON and PDI are complete — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → route vehicle to DHD for full detail ($60) before placing in Cage; notify DHD via [NEEDS_INPUT: DHD contact method]; document routing in WhatsApp team chat; do not move vehicle to Cage until detail is confirmed complete

---

## Failure Action Summary

| Item | Failure Role | Failure Path |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Airtable status incorrect | Lot Manager | Correct status; document in WhatsApp |
| 2 — Wrong zone | Lot Attendant | Move to West Side of Building |
| 3 — No stock-in tag | Lot Attendant | Obtain from Jorja or Giselle; place in bottom-right windshield |
| 4 — No active work order | Lot Manager | Contact Service Department Lead via WhatsApp; request work order assignment |
| 5 — RECON complete but no PDI | Lot Manager | Contact Service Department Lead; vehicle stays in West Side of Building until PDI marked complete |
| 6 — RECON+PDI complete but no detail | Lot Attendant | Route to DHD ($60); do not move to Cage until detail confirmed complete |
