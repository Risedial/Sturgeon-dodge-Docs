# Vehicle Audit Checklist — FLR
**Category:** FLR (Front-Line Ready) — Airtable status AVAILABLE or DEMO — vehicles currently in or being placed in the Cage
**Used by:** Lot Attendant or Lot Manager during morning walk or placement check
**Status:** [COMPLETE]

---

## Purpose

This checklist verifies that a Front-Line Ready vehicle meets all Cage placement requirements. All items are binary pass/fail. Every No has a Failure Action with a named responsible role.

**Critical rule:** No vehicle enters the Cage without: PDI complete + fully detailed + no stickers + no tape + stock-in tag placed + facing outward + adequate door-opening spacing.

---

## Checklist Items

---

[ ] **1. Is PDI marked complete in the manufacturer system by the assigned technician? (verify in manufacturer system — verbal confirmation is not sufficient)**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → remove vehicle from Cage immediately if it has already been placed; route to Service Department Lead via WhatsApp: "Vehicle [VIN] in Cage / scheduled for Cage does not have PDI marked complete in manufacturer system. This vehicle needs PDI before it can be on the front line." Do not return vehicle to Cage until PDI is confirmed complete in the manufacturer system.

---

[ ] **2. Is the vehicle free of visible dirt, dust, and debris on all exterior panels?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → route vehicle to DHD for full detail ($60 per vehicle); notify DHD via [NEEDS_INPUT: DHD contact method]; document routing in WhatsApp team chat: "Vehicle [VIN] routed to DHD for full detail — exterior not clean to Cage standard." Do not place or return vehicle to Cage until detail is confirmed complete and vehicle is re-inspected.

---

[ ] **3. Are all stickers removed from the vehicle (all exterior panels, glass, and trim)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → remove all stickers from the vehicle before Cage placement.

---

[ ] **4. Is all tape removed from the vehicle (all surfaces — body, glass, trim)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → remove all tape from the vehicle before Cage placement.

---

[ ] **5. Is the stock-in tag present in the bottom-right corner of the windshield (white tag, placed by lot team after PDI and detail are complete)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → go to Admin — Stock Tags (Jorja) or Admin/Tech — Stock Tags + Service (Giselle) immediately; obtain the completed white stock-in tag; place it in the bottom-right corner of the windshield; do not use yellow tags (yellow tags look like AutoWorld tags).

---

[ ] **6. Is the vehicle facing outward (toward the street / customer-facing direction)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → reposition the vehicle to face outward before the Cage is considered compliant for this slot.

---

[ ] **7. Is there adequate spacing on both sides of the vehicle so that both doors can fully open for a customer to enter and exit?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → reposition the vehicle within the Cage slot or adjust adjacent vehicles so that doors on both sides can fully open; a customer must be able to fully open a door to inspect and enter the vehicle.

---

[ ] **8. Is the vehicle parked in the correct Cage slot per placement priority rules (C01–C20)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → move vehicle to correct slot per placement priority rules → SEE: vehicle-placement-cage.md; document the repositioning in the WhatsApp team chat.

---

## Pass Criteria

Vehicle passes this checklist only when all 8 items are answered YES. A vehicle with any NO item outstanding is NOT eligible for Cage placement until the Failure Action for that item has been completed and re-verified.

---

## Notes

- `[NEEDS_INPUT]`: DHD contact method — the specific contact (phone, WhatsApp, other) for routing vehicles to DHD for detailing is not confirmed in source material.
- Item 1 (PDI in manufacturer system) is the absolute gate for Cage eligibility. A vehicle without confirmed PDI in the manufacturer system must never be placed in the Cage regardless of any verbal assurances.
- Item 5 (stock-in tag): The tag is placed AFTER PDI is complete AND after detailing is complete. A vehicle being checked into the Cage for the first time should already have its tag placed; a vehicle found in the Cage without a tag represents a tagging compliance failure.
