# Morning Lot Walk Checklist
**Used by:** Lot Manager (or General Manager if Lot Manager is absent)
**Timing:** Approximately 30 minutes after arriving at the lot, after handling any immediate fires
**Duration:** Approximately 30 minutes
**Output:** Task list posted in WhatsApp team chat with tasks grouped by responsible team (Lot, Sales, Service)

---

## Instructions

Complete this checklist every operational day without exception. Every item is answered Yes or No only. Every No triggers the specified Failure Action — do not defer. After completing the walk, generate the task list from all identified issues and post in WhatsApp team chat before the walk is considered complete.

---

## LOT-LEVEL CHECKS — Complete Before Starting Zone Walk

[ ] **1. Are there any staff vehicles currently parked on the lot (not on the street)?**
    NO: Continue
    YES — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → identify the driver using the staff vehicle inventory list; if first-time offense → SEE: staff-parking-new-employee.md; if repeat offense → SEE: staff-parking-repeat-violation.md; document in WhatsApp team chat

[ ] **2. Is the staff vehicle list up to date — does it include name, make, model, and plate number for every current staff member?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → update the staff vehicle list immediately with all missing entries; document the update in management records

---

## ZONE WALK — Cage (C01–C20)

[ ] **3. Is every vehicle in the Cage an FLR or NEW category vehicle — specifically, are there no SOLD, BND, or RECON vehicles in the Cage?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → move the incorrectly placed vehicle to the correct zone per its current status; reference → SEE: vehicle-categorization.md; document the move in WhatsApp team chat

[ ] **4. Does every vehicle in the Cage have the correct signage — specifically, a white stock-in tag placed in the bottom-right corner of the windshield?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → obtain the missing tag from Admin — Stock Tags (Jorja) or Admin/Tech — Stock Tags + Service (Giselle); place tag in bottom-right corner of windshield immediately

[ ] **5. Is every vehicle in the Cage confirmed PDI complete (marked in manufacturer system) AND fully detailed (no stickers, no tape, no visible dirt or debris)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → remove the non-compliant vehicle from the Cage immediately; route to service department if PDI is not confirmed complete; route to DHD ($60 full detail) if detail is not complete; do not return vehicle to Cage until both PDI and detail are confirmed complete

[ ] **6. Are all Cage vehicles facing outward (toward customers/street) with adequate spacing on both sides so that both doors can fully open?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → reposition the non-compliant vehicle(s) so all vehicles face outward and have adequate door-opening spacing

[ ] **7. Is there a maximum of one empty Cage stall — and only if the vehicle in that stall was just sold?**
    YES (no empty stalls, or exactly one stall empty due to a vehicle just being sold): Continue
    NO (more than one empty stall, or an empty stall not explained by a just-sold vehicle) — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → identify the next vehicle to fill the gap; execute Cage placement per → SEE: vehicle-placement-cage.md; document the placement in WhatsApp team chat

---

## ZONE WALK — East Side Fence Line (F1–F5)

[ ] **8. Does every vehicle in the East Side Fence Line have correct signage for its status — specifically: a sold sign with customer name (for SOLD vehicles), or a sold sign with customer name (for BND vehicles), or a stock-in tag (for FLR sedan overflow)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → identify which vehicle is missing signage and its status; route the sign placement task to the appropriate team via WhatsApp team chat with the vehicle description and what sign is needed

[ ] **9. Is every vehicle in the East Side Fence Line correctly categorized for this zone — specifically: SOLD, BND, or FLR sedan overflow only (no RECON, no unprocessed, no non-prime vehicles)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → move the incorrectly placed vehicle to the correct zone per its status; reference → SEE: vehicle-categorization.md; document the move in WhatsApp team chat

---

## ZONE WALK — West Side of Building (L1–L5)

[ ] **10. Is every vehicle in the West Side of Building correctly categorized — specifically: BND overflow, RECON vehicles, or SOLD overflow only (no NEW vehicles, no FLR vehicles, no non-prime vehicles)?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → move the incorrectly placed vehicle to the correct zone per its status; document the move in WhatsApp team chat

[ ] **11. Does every RECON vehicle in the West Side of Building have an active work order assigned in the service department?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → contact Service Department Lead immediately via WhatsApp team chat; SAY: "No work order assigned for RECON vehicle [VIN] in West Side of Building. Please assign a work order immediately."

---

## ZONE WALK — Overflow (Temporary)

[ ] **12. Does every vehicle currently staged in Overflow (Temporary) have a destination zone annotation — specifically, a documented destination in Airtable and/or WhatsApp team chat indicating which permanent zone the vehicle belongs in?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → determine the correct destination zone for each un-annotated vehicle based on its current status; annotate destination in Airtable and post in WhatsApp team chat; if unable to determine status → SEE: vehicle-status-unknown.md

---

## ZONE WALK — Auction Area

[ ] **13. Are all vehicles in the Auction Area correctly designated as auction-bound in Airtable — specifically, is there no retail inventory, FLR, or SOLD vehicle that has been incorrectly staged in the Auction Area?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → investigate the vehicle's correct status; if not auction-bound, identify the correct zone and route vehicle there immediately → SEE: vehicle-status-unknown.md; document findings in WhatsApp team chat

---

## ZONE WALK — Power Sport / Quad Corner

[ ] **14. Does the Power Sport / Quad Corner contain only power sport vehicles, boats, or seasonal Hysen units — specifically, are there no retail vehicles (inventory, SOLD, BND, RECON) in this zone?**
    YES: Continue
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant → move the retail vehicle out of the Power Sport / Quad Corner immediately; route to the correct zone per its current status; document the move in WhatsApp team chat

---

## END OF WALK

[ ] **15. Has a task list been compiled from all identified issues and posted in WhatsApp team chat, with tasks grouped by responsible team (Lot Team, Sales Team, Service Department)?**
    YES: Walk is complete
    NO — Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager → compile the task list from all items found during this walk; post in WhatsApp team chat before ending the walk; group tasks by team: Lot Team tasks (zone corrections, signage, repositioning), Sales Team tasks (sold signs, BND delivery dates), Service Department tasks (PDI assignments, work orders)
