## 1. Morning Lot Walk Checklist

**For use by:** Lot Manager (or General Manager if Lot Manager is absent)
**Timing:** Approximately 30 minutes after arriving at the lot, after handling any immediate fires
**Duration:** Approximately 30 minutes
**Output:** Task list posted in WhatsApp team chat with tasks grouped by responsible team (Lot, Sales, Service)

### Purpose

Mandatory daily physical inspection of every zone and every vehicle on the lot. Identifies compliance failures, signage gaps, zone violations, PDI risks, and unauthorized vehicles. Every issue found during the walk becomes a task assigned to the responsible team.

### Instructions

Complete this checklist every operational day without exception. Every item is answered Yes or No only. Every No triggers the specified Failure Action — do not defer. After completing the walk, generate the task list from all identified issues and post in WhatsApp team chat before the walk is considered complete.

### Section A — Lot-Level Checks (Complete Before Starting Zone Walk)

---

**Item 1 — Staff Vehicle Check**

[ ] Are there any staff vehicles currently parked on the lot (not on the street)?

    NO: ✓ Continue (no action needed)
    YES: → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Identify the driver using the staff vehicle inventory list.
         If first-time offense: → Decision Tree: staff-parking-new-employee.md
         If repeat offense:     → Decision Tree: staff-parking-repeat-violation.md
         Document in WhatsApp team chat.

> ⚠️ NEVER: Allow staff vehicles on the dealership lot. The Owner/General Manager parks on the street — this standard applies to everyone without exception. One exception destroys the standard.

[ ] Is the staff vehicle list up to date — does it include name, make, model, and plate number for every current staff member?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Update the staff vehicle list immediately with all missing entries.
         Document the update in management records.

## 2. PDI Completion Tracking Checklist

**For use by:** Lot Manager — review daily
**Purpose:** Track PDI completion compliance for all vehicles on the lot currently requiring PDI
**Compliance standard:** Stellantis requires final PDI within 2 calendar days of vehicle delivery to the dealership
**Current compliance rate:** **80% — RED ZONE** (fines and docking begin at or near this level)

### Purpose

Maintains a per-vehicle tracking record for every vehicle currently on the lot that requires PDI but has not yet been confirmed complete. Updated daily. Escalation is immediate when any vehicle reaches OVERDUE status — do not wait.

### Instructions

Maintain one row per vehicle in the tracking table below. Remove vehicles only when PDI is confirmed marked complete in the manufacturer system. Apply the status and required action from the Status Definitions table to each vehicle each day.

---

### Per-Vehicle Tracking Record

For each vehicle currently requiring PDI, record the following fields. One row per vehicle.

| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| VIN | ______________________________________________ |
| Vehicle Description (Year / Make / Model) | ______________________________________________ |
| Arrival Date (Day 0) | ______________________________________________ |
| PDI Deadline (Arrival Date + 2 calendar days) | ______________________________________________ |
| PDI Status in Manufacturer System | Pending / In Progress / Complete |
| Today's Date | ______________________________________________ |
| Days Remaining / Overdue (PDI Deadline − Today) | ______________________________________________ |
| Current Status | ON TIME / COMPLETE / DAY 1 OVERDUE / DAY 2+ OVERDUE |

*Positive number = days remaining. Negative number = days overdue.*

---

### Status Definitions and Required Actions

| Status | Condition | Required Action |
|---|---|---|
| ON TIME | Today's date is on or before the PDI deadline AND PDI is not yet marked complete | No escalation required — monitor daily. Confirm with Service Department Lead that PDI is scheduled. |
| COMPLETE | PDI is marked complete in the manufacturer system by the assigned technician | Verify technician logged it in manufacturer system (not just verbal). Remove vehicle from active tracking. Post in WhatsApp: "PDI complete for [VIN] — removing from tracking." Update Airtable status. |
| DAY 1 OVERDUE | PDI deadline has passed by exactly 1 day AND PDI is not marked complete | ROLE: Lot Manager → contact Service Department Lead immediately via WhatsApp. Use Day 1 Overdue escalation language below. Document escalation in WhatsApp. |
| **DAY 2+ OVERDUE (CRITICAL)** | PDI deadline has passed by 2 or more days AND PDI is not marked complete | **ROLE: Lot Manager → escalate to General Manager immediately. Use Day 2+ Overdue language below. Simultaneously escalate to Service Department Lead. Document in WhatsApp.** |

---

### Checklist Item — Per Vehicle

For each vehicle currently in this tracking record:

[ ] Has the PDI been marked complete in the manufacturer system by the assigned technician?

    YES: ✓ COMPLETE — remove from active tracking; update WhatsApp team chat;
         update Airtable as appropriate
    NO:  → Apply the required action from the Status Definitions table above based on
         the vehicle's current status (ON TIME, DAY 1 OVERDUE, or DAY 2+ OVERDUE CRITICAL)

---

### Escalation Language Reference

**Day 1 Overdue — to Service Department Lead (WhatsApp):**
> "PDI for [VIN] is now overdue by 1 day. Stellantis compliance window has passed. Please prioritize this PDI today."

**Day 2+ Overdue — to General Manager (in person or direct message):**
> "PDI for [VIN] is [X] days overdue. We are at risk of a Stellantis compliance fine and potential docking. This needs to be resolved today."

**Day 2+ Overdue — to Service Department Lead (WhatsApp, simultaneously):**
> "PDI for [VIN] is [X] days overdue — compliance critical. Please complete today and mark in manufacturer system."

---

### What "PDI Complete" Means

PDI is complete ONLY when:
1. A service technician has physically performed the full Pre-Delivery Inspection per Stellantis requirements
2. **The technician has marked the PDI complete in the manufacturer system** (not just Airtable, not just verbally)

PDI is **NOT complete** if:
- A technician checked the vehicle visually without logging it in the manufacturer system
- The PDI was performed at the factory or by the originating dealer (the final PDI at this location is still required)
- A salesperson says the vehicle is "good to go"
- Verbal confirmation was given to the Lot Manager without a manufacturer system entry

> ⚠️ NEVER: Accept verbal confirmation as PDI completion. The manufacturer system entry is the only valid record. "Good to go" from a salesperson is not PDI confirmation.

---

### Important Rules

- **No vehicle enters the Cage without PDI confirmed complete.** If a vehicle is in the Cage and PDI cannot be confirmed in the manufacturer system, remove it from the Cage immediately and route to service.
- **No vehicle is delivered to a customer without PDI confirmed complete.** If a customer is requesting delivery before PDI is done, expedite the PDI — do not skip it. The customer waits.
- **The 2-day window runs from the vehicle's original arrival date.** Ship mode clearance does not reset or extend the clock.
- **"Good to go" from a salesperson is not PDI confirmation.** Check the manufacturer system.

## 3. Key and Dealer Plate Accountability Checklist

**For use by:** Lot Manager; anyone handling Key Cafe transactions
**Applies to:** Any sign-out or sign-in of a key or dealer plate; periodic audits
**Key Cafe location:** 3rd floor by Jorja's desk

### Purpose

Ensures every key and dealer plate transaction is conducted through Key Cafe with full logging, and that the custody chain is maintained at all times. Three sub-sections: Section A (Sign-Out Checks), Section B (Sign-In Checks), Section C (Periodic Audit Checks).

### Financial Penalties (All Enforced — Not Theoretical)

| Item | Penalty | Who Pays | Enforcement Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dealer plate (lost) | **$500** | Employee who last signed it out through Key Cafe | **ENFORCED — CONFIRMED PRECEDENT** |
| GPS key tag (lost) | **$25** | Employee who lost it | **ENFORCED** |
| Vehicle keys (lost) | **~50% of replacement cost** | Employee who lost them | **ENFORCED** |

**Confirmed precedent:** An employee at this dealership paid the full **$500** dealer plate penalty. It was not forgiven.

---

### Section A — Sign-Out Checks

*Complete before issuing any key or dealer plate.*

---

**Item 1 — Accountability Agreement**

[ ] Is the employee's signed Accountability Agreement on file?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         STOP. Do not issue the key or plate.
         SAY: "I can't issue keys or plates until you've signed the accountability
         agreement. See [Sales Manager / General Manager] to complete that before
         returning."
         **Do not make exceptions. No access without a signed agreement on file.**

> ⚠️ NEVER: Issue a key or dealer plate to any employee who has not signed the Accountability Agreement. Without the signed agreement, there is no formal acknowledgment of the financial penalties the employee accepts. No agreement on file = no access, no exceptions.

---

**Item 2 — Key Cafe Transaction (Not Peer-to-Peer)**

[ ] Is this sign-out being conducted through Key Cafe — specifically, is this NOT a peer-to-peer handoff?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Redirect the employee to Key Cafe (3rd floor by Jorja's desk).
         If someone is attempting to hand you a key or plate to pass along to them,
         SAY: "You need to check it out through Key Cafe yourself — I can't accept
         it from you."
         Direct them to Key Cafe immediately.

> ⚠️ NEVER: Accept a key or dealer plate directly from a coworker. NEVER hand a key or dealer plate directly to a coworker. If a coworker asks for the plate or key you currently have signed out, say exactly: "No — go back to Key Cafe, I'll check mine in, you sign it out." This is the exact language. There is no alternative acceptable response.
>
> Why this rule is non-negotiable: If you hand a plate to a coworker without going through Key Cafe, Key Cafe still shows you as responsible. If that plate is lost, you pay the **$500** penalty — even though you no longer have it. The custody chain exists to protect you.

---

**Item 3 — Transaction Logged Before Release**

[ ] Is the transaction being logged with the employee's name and timestamp before the item is released?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Log the transaction (employee name + timestamp) before releasing the item.
         Do not release the item until it is logged in Key Cafe.

---

**Item 4 — Item Available**

[ ] Is the requested item currently available — specifically, is it not currently signed out to another employee?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Requesting employee
         Do not request the item from the employee who currently has it peer-to-peer.
         Either:
           (a) Wait for the item to be returned through Key Cafe, OR
           (b) Contact the employee who currently has it and ask them to return it
               through Key Cafe first; then sign it out through Key Cafe yourself.

---

### Section C — Periodic Audit Checks

*Run at the start of each day OR when any item cannot be located.*

---

**Item 7 — Log Reconciliation**

[ ] Does the Key Cafe log account for all items that should currently be signed out — specifically, can every currently-signed-out item be matched to a log entry with an employee name and sign-out timestamp?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Reconcile the log.
         Identify any transactions that are not logged.
         Document the discrepancy in WhatsApp team chat.
         If any item cannot be accounted for after reconciliation: escalate to

---

**Item 8 — GPS Key Tags Attached**

[ ] Are all GPS key tags physically attached to their key sets?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Check the GPS location of the missing tag before declaring it lost.
         If located via GPS: retrieve the tag and reattach it to the correct key set.
         If tag cannot be located after GPS check: initiate lost response.
         → Decision Tree: key-plate-lost-response.md
         **$25** reprogram cost applies and is charged to the employee responsible.

### The Peer-to-Peer Transfer Rule — Absolute

**Never accept a key or dealer plate directly from a coworker.**
**Never hand a key or dealer plate directly to a coworker.**

If a coworker asks for the plate or key you currently have signed out, say exactly:

> **"No — go back to Key Cafe, I'll check mine in, you sign it out."**

This is the exact language. There is no alternative acceptable response.

**Dealer plates and keys are the equivalent of carrying $500 cash. Treat them accordingly.**
