## Section 1 — Your Lot Responsibilities

The sales team has specific obligations to lot operations. When these obligations are not met, the result is vehicles sitting without identification, lot staff unable to move or process vehicles correctly, compliance failures, and operational confusion for the entire team.

**You are responsible for:**
- **Placing a sold sign on every sold vehicle immediately** — the moment the sale is complete
- Communicating vehicle status changes to the lot team via WhatsApp immediately when they occur
- Communicating expected delivery dates for all BND vehicles
- **Never pressuring lot or service staff to skip PDI**
- Parking on the street — never on the lot
- Notifying the lot team immediately when a trade-in is arriving
- Signing the Accountability Agreement before accessing any key or dealer plate

### What Happens When These Responsibilities Are Not Met

- **Missing sold sign:** The lot team cannot identify the vehicle as sold. It may be moved, displayed to customers, or confused with inventory. The 3-strike escalation protocol is initiated — it is directed at the Sales Manager (Kevin) directly.
- **Missing status communication:** The lot team may move a vehicle that is about to be delivered, creating a customer service failure.
- **Skipped PDI:** Stellantis compliance drops further below **80%**. Financial fines and manufacturer docking are triggered. A confirmed incident at this dealership — vehicle delivered in ship mode, died the next day, required tow, **$275** + rework cost — is the confirmed consequence of skipping the process.
- **Parking on the lot:** Creates congestion, blocks large vehicle deliveries, and sets a negative standard that cascades to the whole team. The Owner/General Manager parks on the street — this standard applies to everyone without exception.

## Section 2 — Sold Sign Responsibility

**This is the single most important lot-facing responsibility of the sales team.**

### The Rule

**Place a sold sign immediately when a vehicle is sold.**

- Not at the end of the day
- Not after the paperwork is fully finalized
- Not once the customer has gone home
- Not when someone asks you to do it

**The moment a sale is complete: the sold sign goes in.**

### How to Place a Sold Sign

1. Go upstairs — sold sign supplies and Sharpies are kept upstairs in the dealership building
2. Write the customer's full name on the sign with a Sharpie
3. Place the sign visibly inside the vehicle window

### If the Lot Team Is Asked to Place It

If the Sales Manager (Kevin) or a salesperson requests the lot team to place a sold sign via WhatsApp, include the vehicle VIN or stock number and the customer name so the sign can be written correctly.

### Enforcement

If a sold vehicle does not have a sold sign, the Lot Manager initiates the 3-strike escalation directed at the Sales Manager (Kevin):

| Strike | Language |
|---|---|
| Strike 1 | **"Hey, Kevin, do you mind getting your guys to put a sold sign in there?"** |
| Strike 2 | **"Hey, Kevin, do you mind?"** |
| Strike 3 | **"Kev, killing me, buddy. Can you just tell me the stuff and I'll do it, because we have to be organized."** |
| Expected result | "No, no, buddy, I'm coming down myself. I'll do it myself." |

**The Sales Manager (Kevin) is accountable for this compliance standard for the entire sales team.**

## Section 3 — Vehicle Status Communication

When a deal is signed, communicate to the lot team via the WhatsApp team chat immediately. **Do not wait until later in the day. Do not assume the lot team already knows.**

**Post in WhatsApp team chat immediately when a deal is signed:**

> "Deal signed on [vehicle VIN / stock number]. Customer: [customer name]. Expected delivery: [date]. Trade-in involved: [YES / NO — if YES, include vehicle description]."

This information allows the lot team to:
- Place the sold sign correctly (customer name)
- Route the vehicle to the correct zone (East Side Fence Line)
- Know not to sell the vehicle to another customer
- Know when to have the vehicle ready for delivery

**If you change the expected delivery date after the initial communication:** Post an update in the WhatsApp team chat immediately with the new delivery date and the reason for the change.

## Section 4 — BND Vehicle Communication

When a deal is signed but the vehicle will not be delivered immediately (Booked Not Delivered), communicate the expected delivery date to the lot team.

**What BND means:** The vehicle has a confirmed deal on paper but the customer has not yet taken possession. The vehicle is parked in the East Side Fence Line (or West Side of Building overflow) until delivery occurs.

**Your responsibility:**
- When a deal is signed with a future delivery date: post in WhatsApp team chat immediately with vehicle info and expected delivery date
- If the delivery date changes: post in WhatsApp team chat immediately with the new date

## Section 5 — PDI Cooperation

> ⚠️ NEVER: Tell the lot team or service department to skip PDI. Never represent a vehicle as "good to go" to bypass the inspection.
> A vehicle may not be delivered to a customer until PDI is confirmed complete in the manufacturer system. We are at **80% compliance — in the red zone.** Non-compliance results in Stellantis fines and docking.

### The Requirement

Every vehicle delivered to the Edmonton Office must have its final PDI completed within 2 calendar days of arrival. This is a Stellantis manufacturer requirement. **Current compliance is at 80% — in the red zone where fines and docking begin.** Recovery from this position requires full participation from the sales team.

### If a Customer Is Pushing for Immediate Delivery

If a customer wants their vehicle before PDI is complete, say:

> **"We need to complete the PDI — it's a Stellantis requirement. We're at 80% compliance, in the red zone. I'll get it prioritized."**

Then immediately contact the Lot Manager or Service Department Lead to request priority PDI processing. **The PDI is expedited — it is not skipped.**

**Do not deliver the vehicle until PDI is confirmed complete in the manufacturer system.** Verbal confirmation from a coworker is not sufficient. The PDI must be in the system.

### What Happens When PDI Is Skipped

> **Confirmed incident at this dealership:** A vehicle was delivered to a customer while still in ship mode. The PDI had not been performed. The detail had not been completed. The car died the next day. The customer was stranded. The vehicle had to be towed back. Total cost: **$275** (skipped detail rework) + tow cost + complete rework of the entire preparation process.

**Do not repeat this incident.**

### If a Salesperson Is Directly Blocking PDI

The Lot Team's response to a salesperson claiming the PDI can be skipped:
> *"Stellantis requires final PDI within 2 days. We're at 80% compliance, in the red zone. It needs to go through."*

If a salesperson continues to push back after this response, the Lot Manager escalates to the Sales Manager (Kevin) and reports the situation. **Kevin is accountable for stopping PDI override attempts from his sales staff.**

## Section 6 — Staff Parking

**All sales staff park on the street. No exceptions.**

### The Rule

Every sales staff member — including the Sales Manager (Kevin) — parks on the street, not on the dealership lot.

- **No exceptions for "just two minutes"**
- **No exceptions for "big trucks are in the way"**
- **No exceptions because you are a manager or senior staff member**
- The Owner/General Manager parks on the street personally — this sets the standard for everyone

### Why This Rule Exists

- The lot has limited space. Inventory vehicles, large delivery trucks, and daily lot operations require every slot.
- Staff vehicles on the lot cannot be distinguished from inventory without checking the staff vehicle inventory list — this wastes the lot team's time every morning.
- One exception creates the expectation that exceptions are possible. **Standards collapse through exceptions.**

### If the Lot Is Blocked or Full

Park on the nearest available street and walk. There is no scenario in which the lot becomes a staff parking area.

## Section 7 — Trade-In Handoff

When you receive a trade-in from a customer, the lot team must be notified immediately so the vehicle can be properly identified and processed.

### Step-by-Step

1. **Notify the lot team via WhatsApp team chat immediately** when a customer trade-in is confirmed or arriving:

   > "Trade-in incoming: [year, make, model, plate/description]. Customer: [customer name]. Initial condition: [brief — e.g., 'clean, needs minor detail' or 'mechanical issues, likely recon']."

2. The lot team places the trade-in banner on the vehicle immediately upon receipt.

3. The routing decision (detail only → DHD; mechanical work needed → West Side of Building recon; auction-bound → Auction Area) is made by sales/management — the lot team executes the physical routing once they have the instruction.

### What NOT to Do

> ⚠️ NEVER: Park a trade-in in the Cage. Trade-ins have not been processed, PDI'd, or detailed.

> ⚠️ NEVER: Park a trade-in in an unmarked location without notifying the lot team. An unidentified vehicle on the lot creates exactly the scenario that paralyzed operations for 6 weeks (the Kia incident).

For the full trade-in processing decision tree:
→ Decision Tree: vehicle-trade-in-processing.md

## Section 8 — Accountability Agreement

**Every sales staff member must sign the Accountability Agreement before accessing any key or dealer plate at the Edmonton Office.**

### What This Means for You

- Before your first key sign-out or dealer plate sign-out: the signed agreement must be on file
- Without the signed agreement: you cannot take a plate, cannot drive a dealership vehicle, cannot access Key Cafe for sign-out

### Why It Matters

The Accountability Agreement formally acknowledges the financial penalties you are accepting responsibility for:
- **$500** if a dealer plate you signed out is lost
- **$25** if a GPS key tag is lost
- **~50% of replacement cost** if a set of keys is lost

**These penalties are enforced. An employee at this dealership paid the $500 plate penalty. It was not forgiven.**

### Key Cafe Rules (Summary)

- **All keys and dealer plates go through Key Cafe — no exceptions**
- **Never take a plate or key from a coworker's hand**
- **Never pass a plate or key to a coworker's hand**
- If a coworker asks you for your plate or key, say: *"No — go back to Key Cafe, I'll check mine in, you sign it out."*
- Sign in your plate or key immediately when you are done with it

Key Cafe is located on the 3rd floor by Jorja's desk.
