## 1. Checklist: NEW Vehicle Audit

**For use by:** Lot Attendant or Lot Manager
**Vehicle category:** NEW — KM ≤ 1,000; vehicles that have not yet reached front-line status
**When to use:** During morning lot walk or arrival processing for any vehicle with KM ≤ 1,000

### Purpose

Verifies that a NEW vehicle meets all pre-front-line standards before it can be moved toward Cage placement. All items are binary pass/fail. Every No has a Failure Action with a named responsible role.

---

### Items

---

**Item 1 — Ship Mode**

[ ] Is ship mode cleared? (battery reconnected AND software reset performed)

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Do not attempt to move or drive the vehicle.
         Notify service department immediately via WhatsApp:
         "Ship mode not cleared on [VIN]. Battery needs reconnection and software reset
         before PDI can begin."
         Do not move vehicle to Cage or deliver to customer.
         Do not proceed to any other checklist item until service department confirms
         ship mode is cleared.

---

**Item 2 — PDI Status**

[ ] Is PDI scheduled or already complete? (verify in manufacturer system — **verbal confirmation is not sufficient**)

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Contact Service Department Lead via WhatsApp immediately.
         SAY: "PDI needed for [VIN]. Stellantis 2-day window is running from
         [arrival date]. Please schedule and assign today."
         Document the request and the arrival date in the WhatsApp team chat.

---

**Item 3 — Airtable Status**

[ ] Is the Airtable system status set to "Pending PDI" — specifically NOT "IN RECON" or any other incorrect status?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Correct Airtable status to the appropriate pre-PDI status immediately.
         **NEVER use "IN RECON" for a new arrival — this hides the vehicle from
         the sales inventory system.**
         Document the correction in WhatsApp team chat:
         "Status corrected for [VIN] — was incorrectly set to [wrong status],
         corrected to Pending PDI."

> ⚠️ NEVER: Apply the "IN RECON" status to a new vehicle arrival. This classification hides the vehicle from the sales inventory system, causing it to be invisible to the sales team for weeks or permanently. New arrivals that have not yet been inspected are "Pending PDI" — not RECON.

---

**Item 4 — Stock-In Tag**

[ ] Is the stock-in tag filled out and placed in the bottom-right corner of the windshield?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Go to Admin — Stock Tags (Jorja) or Admin/Tech — Stock Tags + Service (Giselle)
         immediately.
         Obtain the completed white stock-in tag.
         Place it in the bottom-right corner of the windshield.
         **Do not use yellow tags — yellow tags look like AutoWorld tags and cause
         identification confusion.**

---

**Item 5 — Sticker Removal**

[ ] Are all manufacturer and transport stickers removed from the vehicle (all exterior panels and glass)?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Remove all manufacturer and transport stickers from the vehicle before any
         further processing.

---

**Item 6 — Tape Removal**

[ ] Is all tape removed from the vehicle (all surfaces — body, glass, trim)?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Remove all tape from the vehicle before any further processing.

---

**Item 7 — Exterior Cleanliness**

[ ] Is the vehicle free of visible dirt, dust, and debris on all exterior panels?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Route vehicle to DHD for full detail ($60 per vehicle).
         Notify DHD via [NEEDS_INPUT: DHD contact method].
         Document routing in WhatsApp team chat:
         "Vehicle [VIN] routed to DHD for full detail — exterior not clean to standard."
         Do not place vehicle in Cage until detail is confirmed complete and vehicle
         is re-inspected.

---

### Pass Criteria

Vehicle passes this checklist only when **all 7 items are answered YES**. A vehicle with any NO item outstanding is **NOT eligible for Cage placement** until the Failure Action for that item has been completed and re-verified.

### Notes

- `[NEEDS_INPUT]`: DHD contact method — the specific contact (phone, WhatsApp, or other) for routing vehicles to DHD for detailing is not confirmed in source material.
- The 2-day PDI window (Stellantis requirement) runs from the vehicle's arrival date at the Edmonton Office — not from the date ship mode is cleared or the date this checklist is run.
- → Decision Tree: vehicle-arrival-new-standard.md
- → Decision Tree: vehicle-arrival-ship-mode.md

## 2. Checklist: FLR (Frontline Ready) Vehicle Audit

**For use by:** Lot Attendant or Lot Manager
**Vehicle category:** FLR (Front-Line Ready) — Airtable status AVAILABLE or DEMO; vehicles currently in or being placed in the Cage
**When to use:** During morning lot walk or during Cage placement check for any AVAILABLE or DEMO vehicle

### Purpose

Verifies that a Front-Line Ready vehicle meets all Cage placement requirements. All items are binary pass/fail. Every No has a Failure Action with a named responsible role.

**Critical rule:** **No vehicle enters the Cage without: PDI complete + fully detailed + no stickers + no tape + stock-in tag placed + facing outward + adequate door-opening spacing.**

---

### Items

---

**Item 1 — PDI Confirmation (Cage Gate)**

[ ] Is PDI marked complete in the manufacturer system by the assigned technician? (verify in manufacturer system — **verbal confirmation is not sufficient**)

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Remove vehicle from Cage immediately if it has already been placed.
         Route to Service Department Lead via WhatsApp:
         "Vehicle [VIN] in Cage / scheduled for Cage does not have PDI marked complete
         in manufacturer system. This vehicle needs PDI before it can be on the
         front line."
         Do not return vehicle to Cage until PDI is confirmed complete in the
         manufacturer system.

> ⚠️ NEVER: Place a vehicle in the Cage without PDI confirmed complete in the manufacturer system. A salesperson saying the vehicle is "good to go" is NOT confirmation. The manufacturer system entry is the only valid record of PDI completion. Current compliance is at 80% — in the red zone where Stellantis fines and docking begin.

---

**Item 2 — Exterior Cleanliness**

[ ] Is the vehicle free of visible dirt, dust, and debris on all exterior panels?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Route vehicle to DHD for full detail ($60 per vehicle).
         Notify DHD via [NEEDS_INPUT: DHD contact method].
         Document routing in WhatsApp team chat:
         "Vehicle [VIN] routed to DHD for full detail — exterior not clean to Cage
         standard."
         Do not place or return vehicle to Cage until detail is confirmed complete
         and vehicle is re-inspected.

---

**Item 3 — Sticker Removal**

[ ] Are all stickers removed from the vehicle (all exterior panels, glass, and trim)?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Remove all stickers from the vehicle before Cage placement.

---

**Item 4 — Tape Removal**

[ ] Is all tape removed from the vehicle (all surfaces — body, glass, trim)?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Remove all tape from the vehicle before Cage placement.

---

**Item 5 — Stock-In Tag**

[ ] Is the stock-in tag present in the bottom-right corner of the windshield? (white tag, placed by lot team after PDI and detail are complete)

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Go to Admin — Stock Tags (Jorja) or Admin/Tech — Stock Tags + Service (Giselle)
         immediately.
         Obtain the completed white stock-in tag.
         Place it in the bottom-right corner of the windshield.
         **Do not use yellow tags — yellow tags look like AutoWorld tags.**

---

**Item 6 — Vehicle Orientation**

[ ] Is the vehicle facing outward (toward the street / customer-facing direction)?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Reposition the vehicle to face outward before the Cage is considered compliant
         for this slot.

---

**Item 7 — Door Spacing**

[ ] Is there adequate spacing on both sides of the vehicle so that both doors can fully open for a customer to enter and exit?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Reposition the vehicle within the Cage slot or adjust adjacent vehicles so that
         doors on both sides can fully open.
         A customer must be able to fully open a door to inspect and enter the vehicle.

---

**Item 8 — Slot Assignment**

[ ] Is the vehicle parked in the correct Cage slot per placement priority rules (C01–C20)?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Move vehicle to correct slot per placement priority rules.
         → Decision Tree: vehicle-placement-cage.md
         Document the repositioning in the WhatsApp team chat.

---

### Pass Criteria

Vehicle passes this checklist only when **all 8 items are answered YES**. A vehicle with any NO item outstanding is **NOT eligible for Cage placement** until the Failure Action for that item has been completed and re-verified.

### Notes

- `[NEEDS_INPUT]`: DHD contact method — specific contact for routing vehicles to DHD is not confirmed in source material.
- Item 1 (PDI in manufacturer system) is the **absolute gate** for Cage eligibility. A vehicle without confirmed PDI in the manufacturer system must never be placed in the Cage regardless of any verbal assurances.
- Item 5 (stock-in tag): The tag is placed AFTER PDI is complete AND after detailing is complete. A vehicle found in the Cage without a tag represents a tagging compliance failure.
- → Decision Tree: vehicle-placement-cage.md
- → Decision Tree: vehicle-pdi-compliance-deadline.md

## 3. Checklist: SOLD Vehicle Audit

**For use by:** Lot Attendant or Lot Manager
**Vehicle category:** SOLD — Airtable status SIGNED DEAL or WHOLESALE|SOLD
**When to use:** During morning lot walk for every SOLD vehicle on the lot

### Purpose

Verifies that a SOLD vehicle is correctly identified, correctly parked, and that PDI is on track before delivery. All items are binary pass/fail. Every No has a Failure Action with a named responsible role.

---

### Items

---

**Item 1 — Sold Sign**

[ ] Is a sold sign present in the vehicle with the customer's name written on it?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Initiate the 3-strike escalation protocol directed at Sales Manager (Kevin).
         → Decision Tree: sold-sign-missing-enforcement.md

         Strike 1 — SAY to Kevin:
         "Hey, Kevin, do you mind getting your guys to put a sold sign in there?"

         Strike 2 — SAY to Kevin (if not resolved after Strike 1):
         "Hey, Kevin, do you mind?"

         Strike 3 — SAY to Kevin (if not resolved after Strike 2):
         "Kev, killing me, buddy. Can you just tell me the stuff and I'll do it,
         because we have to be organized."

         If salesperson cannot be reached at any strike level and sign remains missing:
         ROLE: Lot Manager → obtain customer name from Sales Manager (Kevin);
         get sold sign from upstairs (Sharpies available at same location);
         write customer name on sign; place sign visibly inside the vehicle.
         Document the missing sign and corrective action in WhatsApp team chat.

> ⚠️ NEVER: Leave a sold vehicle without a sold sign. The Kia incident — a vehicle that sat on the Edmonton Office lot for 6 weeks with no identification — is the confirmed consequence of unidentified vehicles. Every sold vehicle must have a sold sign with the customer's name the moment the sale is complete.

---

**Item 2 — Zone Compliance**

[ ] Is the vehicle parked in the East Side Fence Line (F1–F5) or West Side of Building (L1–L5) — NOT in the Cage or any front-line zone?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Move vehicle out of the Cage or incorrect zone immediately.
         Primary destination: East Side Fence Line (slots F1–F5).
         If East Side Fence Line is full: move to West Side of Building (slots L1–L5).
         If both zones are full: move to Overflow (Temporary); annotate destination
         in Airtable and WhatsApp team chat.
         Document the move in WhatsApp.

---

**Item 3 — PDI Before Delivery**

[ ] Is PDI confirmed complete in the manufacturer system before the vehicle is delivered to the customer?

    YES (or delivery is not yet scheduled — no action required): ✓ Continue
    NO AND delivery is imminent (customer has a confirmed delivery appointment or
    is on their way):
    → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
      Do NOT allow delivery to proceed.
      Contact Service Department Lead immediately via WhatsApp:
      "PDI for [VIN] has not been marked complete in the manufacturer system.
      Delivery cannot proceed. Stellantis requires PDI completion within 2 days
      of vehicle arrival. Please prioritize PDI now."
      Escalate to General Manager immediately if PDI cannot be expedited before
      the customer arrives.
      → Decision Tree: vehicle-pdi-compliance-deadline.md
      Document the situation in WhatsApp.

> ⚠️ NEVER: Deliver a vehicle to a customer without PDI confirmed complete in the manufacturer system. Confirmed real incident at this dealership: a vehicle was delivered while still in ship mode. The car died the next day, the customer was stranded, and the vehicle required a tow back at a cost of $275 (detail rework) + tow cost + complete rework. Do not repeat this incident.

---

### Pass Criteria

Vehicle passes this checklist only when all applicable items are answered YES. Item 3 is conditional — if no delivery is imminent, it is a monitoring item only. A SOLD vehicle with a missing sold sign (Item 1 NO) or incorrect parking (Item 2 NO) requires **immediate corrective action** regardless of delivery status.

### Notes

- Sold sign supplies are kept upstairs in the dealership building. Sharpies are kept with the sold signs.
- `[NEEDS_INPUT]`: Formal escalation consequence if Strike 3 of the 3-strike sold sign protocol fails to produce the sold sign — not confirmed in source material.
- PDI compliance: Current rate is **80%** — the Edmonton Office is in the red zone where Stellantis fines and docking begin.

## 5. Checklist: RECON Vehicle Audit

**For use by:** Lot Attendant or Lot Manager
**Vehicle category:** RECON — Airtable status: IN RECON, INCOMING, WHOLESALE, or CHASE
**Zone:** West Side of Building (L1–L5)
**When to use:** During morning lot walk for every vehicle currently classified as RECON on the lot

### Purpose

Verifies that a RECON vehicle is correctly classified, correctly placed, properly tagged, and has an active work order. Verifies the sequential completion gate before any RECON vehicle can be moved to the Cage. All items are binary pass/fail. Every No has a Failure Action with a named responsible role.

---

### Items

---

**Item 1 — Airtable Status**

[ ] Is the Airtable system status correct for a RECON vehicle — one of: IN RECON, INCOMING, WHOLESALE, or CHASE — and NOT "Pending PDI" unless it is a new arrival whose PDI revealed mechanical issues?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Correct Airtable status immediately.
         If uncertain what status to apply: → Decision Tree: vehicle-categorization.md
         Document correction in WhatsApp team chat.

---

**Item 2 — Zone Compliance**

[ ] Is the vehicle parked in the West Side of Building (slots L1–L5)?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Move vehicle to West Side of Building immediately.
         Document move in WhatsApp team chat.

---

**Item 3 — Stock-In Tag**

[ ] Is a stock-in tag (white) present in the bottom-right corner of the vehicle windshield?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
         Obtain stock-in tag from Admin — Stock Tags (Jorja) or Admin/Tech — Stock Tags
         + Service (Giselle).
         Place tag in bottom-right corner of windshield.
         Document in WhatsApp team chat.

---

**Item 4 — Work Order**

[ ] Is there an active work order or recon task assigned to this vehicle in the service department?

    YES: ✓ Continue
    NO:  → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
         Contact Service Department Lead immediately via WhatsApp team chat.
         SAY: "No work order assigned for RECON vehicle [VIN]. Please assign a
         work order immediately."

---

**Item 5 — PDI After RECON**

[ ] If RECON work is complete: is PDI also confirmed complete in the manufacturer system? (not verbal — check system)

    YES (PDI confirmed complete in manufacturer system): ✓ Continue
    YES (RECON is not yet complete — this check does not apply yet): ✓ Continue
    NO and RECON is complete:
    → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Manager
      Contact Service Department Lead immediately via WhatsApp team chat.
      SAY: "RECON is complete for [VIN] but PDI is not yet marked complete in the
      manufacturer system. Route to PDI immediately — do not move vehicle to Cage
      until PDI is complete."
      Vehicle stays in West Side of Building until PDI is marked complete in system.

---

**Item 6 — Detail Before Cage**

[ ] If both RECON and PDI are complete: is full detail (DHD, **$60**) also confirmed complete before moving to the Cage?

    YES (detail confirmed complete): ✓ Continue — vehicle may be moved to Cage
    YES (RECON and/or PDI not yet complete — this check does not apply yet): ✓ Continue
    NO and both RECON and PDI are complete:
    → Failure Action: ROLE: Lot Attendant
      Route vehicle to DHD for full detail ($60) before placing in Cage.
      Notify DHD via [NEEDS_INPUT: DHD contact method].
      Document routing in WhatsApp team chat.
      Do not move vehicle to Cage until detail is confirmed complete.

---

### Failure Action Summary

| Item | Failure Role | Failure Path |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Airtable status incorrect | Lot Manager | Correct status; document in WhatsApp |
| 2 — Wrong zone | Lot Attendant | Move to West Side of Building |
| 3 — No stock-in tag | Lot Attendant | Obtain from Jorja or Giselle; place in bottom-right windshield |
| 4 — No active work order | Lot Manager | Contact Service Department Lead via WhatsApp; request work order assignment |
| 5 — RECON complete but no PDI | Lot Manager | Contact Service Department Lead; vehicle stays in West Side of Building until PDI marked complete |
| 6 — RECON+PDI complete but no detail | Lot Attendant | Route to DHD (**$60**); do not move to Cage until detail confirmed complete |

### Pass Criteria

Vehicle passes this checklist when all applicable items are answered YES. A RECON vehicle may only move to the Cage after Items 1–6 are all confirmed — Airtable status correct, West Side of Building placement, stock-in tag present, work order active, PDI complete in manufacturer system, and full detail complete.
